## Installing on DigitalOcean

Before you start, review the [requirements](./requisitos).

<Steps>
<Step>

### Create the Droplet

In the DigitalOcean dashboard, create a new **Droplet** with Ubuntu LTS, choose the region closest to your team and add your SSH key as the authentication method.

</Step>
<Step>

### Configure the firewall

Create a **Cloud Firewall** in DigitalOcean and assign it to the Droplet, allowing only incoming SSH (22), HTTP (80) and HTTPS (443) traffic.

</Step>
<Step>

### Point your domain

Create a DNS record of type `A` that points your domain or subdomain to the Droplet's public IP. You can do this in DigitalOcean (**Networking → Domains**) or with the provider where you manage your domain.

</Step>
<Step>

### Install DivisionCero

Connect to the Droplet over SSH and follow the installation instructions in the [DivisionCero repository](https://github.com/PetterVargas/divisioncero).

</Step>
<Step>

### Activate your license

If you have a paid plan, activate the license provided by DivisionCero. This step isn't needed with the Community edition.

</Step>
<Step>

### Enable backups

In the Droplet settings, enable DigitalOcean's automatic **Backups**.

</Step>
</Steps>
