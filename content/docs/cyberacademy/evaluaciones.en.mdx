## Passing Criteria

To pass a course assessment you need to:

- ✅ **Get at least 80% of answers correct**
- ✅ **Complete all the course modules**
- ✅ **Finish the required hands-on projects**

## Assessment Structure

### Question Types

Assessments include different formats:

- **Multiple choice**: Selecting the correct answers
- **Practical scenarios**: Analyzing real cases
- **Vulnerability identification**: Detecting security problems
- **Incident resolution**: Responding to cybersecurity situations
- **Secure configuration**: Identifying best practices

### Duration

All assessments have a 60-minute time limit.

## Number of Attempts

- 🔁 **2 attempts** per assessment
- 💳 **Additional attempts**: if you need more, you can get another 2 attempts for an additional fee
- ⏳ **Waiting period**: 24 hours between failed attempts
- 📈 **Learning progression**: Each attempt includes new questions

## Technical Requirements

To take the assessments you need:

- 💻 An up-to-date browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)
- 🌐 A stable internet connection
- 📱 A device with a screen of at least 10 inches (recommended)
- 🔐 An active session on your CyberAcademy account

## Academic Integrity

Assessments are designed to:

- 🔒 Prevent random answers with a dynamic question bank
- 👤 Validate the student's identity
- ⚖️ Ensure fairness in assessment
- 🎓 Ensure the certification reflects real knowledge

## Preparing for the Assessment

### Recommendations

- ✓ Complete all hands-on projects before the assessment
- ✓ Review the key concepts of each module
- ✓ Practice in lab environments when available
- ✓ Read the feedback from previous exercises
- ✓ Make sure you have a quiet environment during the assessment

### Study Resources

- 📚 Full course material
- 🎥 Explanatory videos for each module
- 💼 Real case studies
- 🧪 Hands-on labs (where applicable)
- 📝 Summaries and reference guides

## After the Assessment

Once you pass with **80% or more**:

- 🎉 Immediate access to request your **certification**
- 📊 Detailed report of your areas of strength
- 📈 Continuous learning recommendations
- 🏆 Digital course completion badge
