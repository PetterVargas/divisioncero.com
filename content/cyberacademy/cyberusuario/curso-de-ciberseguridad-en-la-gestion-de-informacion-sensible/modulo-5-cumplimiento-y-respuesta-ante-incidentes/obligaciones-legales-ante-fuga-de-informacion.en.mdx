---
title: "Legal Obligations in the Event of an Information Leak"
description: "What the law requires when data leaks"
---

When an information leak involving personal data occurs, most data protection laws require the organization to notify the competent authorities and, in many cases, the affected individuals too, within deadlines that are usually quite short, sometimes just a few hours or a few days from when the incident is detected. This obligation doesn't depend on whether the leak was intentional or accidental, so a file sent by mistake to the wrong person can trigger the same legal liabilities as a deliberate attack if the content included sensitive personal data. As an employee, this means that immediately reporting any suspected leak, without trying to resolve it or hide it on your own, is essential for the organization to meet these tight legal deadlines. Delaying the report, even for a few hours while you try to confirm whether the problem is real, can turn a manageable incident into a legal breach with much more serious consequences for everyone.
