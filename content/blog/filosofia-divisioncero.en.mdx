## Philosophy

Let's build technology that is *trustworthy* — not just designed to work or to make attacks harder.

Let's write code made to be *shared*, not hidden.

Every line of code is a link in a global chain with the potential to *improve people's lives*.

Let's stand together against the *ego in cybersecurity* that has affected all of us, often leading to the creation of *redundant frameworks, standards, benchmarks and guidelines* — each focused on its own vision, without really aiming to co-create, unify or contribute to a world with fewer people or organizations under attack.
This fragmentation has resulted in decades of *best practices* that, while valid, remain disconnected from *real, widespread applicability*.  
*True progress* will only come when we stop competing and start collaborating for cybersecurity that is *more accessible and effective for everyone*.

Finally, let's always remember that *technology exists to serve people*.  
We must not let technological progress *dehumanize* us or pull us away from our essential purpose: creating tools that *empower, protect and uplift people* in their everyday lives.  

Cybersecurity is, above all, an *ethical responsibility* toward those who trust us to safeguard their *privacy, their rights and their freedom in cyberspace*.
