## What is this course for?

Methodologies for identifying and mitigating threats from the design stage.

## What will I learn?

- 🚀 **Threat Modeling Fundamentals**: Learn what threat modeling is and the roles involved in the exercise.
- 🚀 **Threat Modeling Methodologies**: Apply methodologies such as STRIDE, PASTA, DREAD and Attack Trees.
- 🚀 **The Threat Modeling Process**: Decompose the application and identify threats and attack surfaces.
- 🚀 **Mitigation and Follow-up**: Define controls and follow up on identified threats.
- 🚀 **Threat Modeling at Scale**: Automate and scale threat modeling in agile development environments.

## Projects

With this course you'll create, optimize, automate or improve the following projects:

- 🚀 You'll develop applications with secure coding and threat modeling practices.
