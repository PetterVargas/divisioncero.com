## Frequently asked questions

<Accordions>
  <Accordion title="Is Self-hosting open source?">
    Yes. The source code is public on [GitHub](https://github.com/PetterVargas/divisioncero) and distributed under the [DivisionCero License](./licencia), our own open-source license. The Community edition is free for organizations that meet its conditions; if you modify the code, you must publish your changes under the same license.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I use the Community edition in my company?">
    Yes, if your organization has up to 9 employees and its annual revenue doesn't exceed 100K USD. If you don't meet either condition, you need a [paid plan](./planes).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Which infrastructure providers are supported?">
    DigitalOcean and Hostinger. See the [DigitalOcean](./instalacion-en-digitalocean) and [Hostinger](./instalacion-en-hostinger) guides.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does the price include the server?">
    No. The plans cover the DivisionCero license; the server is billed directly by DigitalOcean or Hostinger.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Which plan includes Single Sign-On (SSO)?">
    The **Company of 50+** plan.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What's the difference from CSaaS?">
    With CSaaS, DivisionCero runs the platform for you. With Self-hosting, your organization installs and runs it on its own infrastructure.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Who is responsible for backups and updates?">
    Your organization. Review the responsibilities in the [DivisionCero License](./licencia).
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>
