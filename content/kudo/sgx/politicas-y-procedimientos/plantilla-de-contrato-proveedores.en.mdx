## Purpose
Provide a base structure that Legal and Purchasing use to draft vendor contracts, ensuring the applicable [Vendor Clauses](/en/kudo/sgx/politicas-y-procedimientos/clausulas-para-proveedores) and [Terms and Conditions for Vendors](/en/kudo/sgx/politicas-y-procedimientos/terminos-y-condiciones-para-proveedores) are incorporated.

## Scope
Applies as a starting point for contracts with vendors classified as Medium, High or Critical. Legal adjusts the template according to the specific nature of the service.

## Contract Structure

**[Service Agreement / Supply of Goods] Contract**

Between **[Organization's Legal Name]**, with tax ID **[●]**, hereinafter "the Organization", and **[Vendor's Legal Name]**, with tax ID **[●]**, hereinafter "the Vendor", it is agreed:

### Clause One – Representations
Each party represents its current legal existence, the capacity of its representatives to sign the contract, and that it has completed the Vendor Onboarding Form where applicable.

### Clause Two – Purpose
Description of the goods or service to be provided, with reference to the Purchase Request that originated the contracting.

### Clause Three – Vendor Obligations
Service-specific obligations, plus the general obligations incorporated by reference from the Vendor Clauses (confidentiality, intellectual property, data protection, information security, legal compliance and anti-corruption, subcontracting, service levels, audit, liability, termination) and from the Terms and Conditions for Vendors applicable to the service's domain.

### Clause Four – Organization Obligations
Timely payments, delivery of the necessary inputs or access, and communication channels for support or incidents.

### Clause Five – Value and Payment Terms
Total value or rates, invoicing conditions and payment terms.

### Clause Six – Term and Validity
Start date, duration and renewal or extension conditions.

### Clause Seven – Intellectual Property
The Intellectual Property clause of the [Vendor Clauses](/en/kudo/sgx/politicas-y-procedimientos/clausulas-para-proveedores) is incorporated in full.

### Clause Eight – Data Protection and Information Security
The corresponding clauses of the Vendor Clauses and the applicable DIA and CIP domain Terms and Conditions are incorporated.

### Clause Nine – Service Levels (SLA)
Availability indicators, response times and penalties for non-compliance, where applicable.

### Clause Ten – Subcontracting
Conditions under which the Vendor may subcontract, in accordance with the corresponding clause of the Vendor Clauses.

### Clause Eleven – Termination
Grounds for early termination, including breach of the Vendor Clauses or the Terms and Conditions, and the procedure for returning information and assets.

### Clause Twelve – Dispute Resolution and Governing Law
Direct settlement mechanism and, failing that, the forum and governing law agreed by the parties.

### Clause Thirteen – Notices
Contact details and valid means for notices between the parties.

### Signatures
Space for the signatures of both parties' legal representatives, with the date and place of signing.

## Monitoring and Review
Legal reviews this template annually, or sooner if the Vendor Clauses, the Terms and Conditions or the applicable regulations change.
