---
title: "Reference Regulatory Framework (ISO 22301)"
description: "The international standard that structures continuity"
---

ISO 22301 is the international reference standard for business continuity management systems, and its practical value goes beyond obtaining a certification: it offers a proven structure for the organization to identify its critical processes, define recovery objectives and keep the plan alive through periodic reviews instead of a document that's filed away after being created. Adopting this framework matters because it standardizes the language across areas, so everyone understands the same thing by business impact analysis or recovery time objective, which makes audits, negotiations with insurers and responses to customers who require evidence of operational resilience as a contractual condition easier. A relevant nuance is that complying with the standard doesn't guarantee the plan will work in a real event: certification validates the management process, but only periodic testing and drills show whether the organization can really recover within the defined time frames.
