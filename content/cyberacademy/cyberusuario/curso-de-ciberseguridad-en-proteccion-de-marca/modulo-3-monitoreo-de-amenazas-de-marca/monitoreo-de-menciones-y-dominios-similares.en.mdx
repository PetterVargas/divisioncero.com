---
title: "Monitoring Mentions and Lookalike Domains"
description: "Watching mentions and domains similar to the brand"
---

Monitoring mentions and similar domains means continuously watching what's said about the brand on the internet and detecting websites that imitate the official name with small variations, such as changing a letter or adding a hyphen. This practice matters because attackers register these lookalike domains to launch phishing campaigns or sell fake products, taking advantage of the trust customers already have in the brand. Automated monitoring tools can track thousands of new domain registrations every day and alert you when a suspicious one appears, for example when someone registers "yourcompany-support.com" to pose as the real customer service and ask unsuspecting users who trust the familiar name for bank details.
