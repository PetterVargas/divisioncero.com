---
title: "Difference between Compliance-Based and Risk-Based Auditing"
description: "Auditing by checklist or by real exposure"
---

A compliance-based audit verifies that the organization meets the requirements of a specific standard or framework, point by point, without paying much attention to which of those points represents the greatest real exposure. A risk-based audit, on the other hand, prioritizes what to review according to the potential impact and the likelihood of something failing, spending more time on critical assets and processes and less on low-risk ones. In practice, it's worth combining both approaches, since compliance alone can create a false sense of security, marking as passed a control that technically exists but is weak or easy to bypass in real operations. An experienced auditor uses the regulatory framework as a baseline, but adjusts the scope and depth of testing according to the specific risk of the business being audited.
