Friends, the one speaking to you isn't an adult; it's the 10- and 13-year-old kid who had two wonderful opportunities:

- The first, *a neighbor who selflessly taught him how to use a computer*
- The second, *one of his best friends let him stay at his house every afternoon, using their computer (it was literally almost 2 years, hehe)*

I truly believe in sharing what life has taught me and making it accessible; and please don't take this as the typical startup buzzword 
*"...democratizing..."*; the projects are currently funded with my own resources, but I do want them to become self-sustaining.
