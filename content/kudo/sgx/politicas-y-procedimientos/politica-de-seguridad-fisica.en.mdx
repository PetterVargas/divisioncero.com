title: "Physical Security Policy"
description: "Physical protection of facilities, equipment and assets"
---

## Purpose
Protect the organization's facilities, assets and information against theft, unauthorized access and physical and environmental threats, ensuring business continuity, employee well-being and legal compliance.

## Scope
Applies to employees, contractors, visitors and third parties with physical access to the organization's facilities or assets, both on-site and off-premises, worldwide.

## Key Policy and Controls

**Perimeter Security**
- Fences, barriers and entrances designed according to risk assessment and asset criticality.

**Facility Access Control**
- Access limited to authorized staff, with electronic logging.
- Visitors always escorted.

**Protection of Offices and Critical Infrastructure**
- Access controls differentiated by location.
- Avoid external signage indicating critical areas.

**Physical Surveillance**
- CCTV on perimeters, entrances, loading docks and sensitive areas, continuously monitored by security staff.

**Protection against Physical and Environmental Threats**
- Response plans for natural disasters, explosives and other threats, reviewed and practiced regularly.

**Secure Asset Handling**
- Secure storage of assets and mandatory device locking when idle.

**Cabling Security**
- Cables protected against damage or interference; separation of power and data; additional controls on critical systems (armored conduits, locked rooms, electromagnetic shielding, periodic inspections).
- Physical labeling to identify cables.

**Storage Media Management**
- Secure lifecycle: acquisition, use, transport and disposal.
- Cryptography for confidential data, redundant copies and a log of removable media.
- Secure deletion and physical destruction of media where appropriate.

**Security of Off-Site Assets**
- Authorization and logging of assets taken off-premises.
- Protection of equipment in transit and in use in public places.
- Access control, tracking and remote wipe measures where possible.
- Additional protocols for equipment permanently installed off-premises.

**Secure Asset Disposal**
- Media containing sensitive data are securely erased or destroyed before disposal or reuse.

## Compliance
All staff and entities involved must comply with this policy. Non-compliance may lead to disciplinary action.

## Monitoring and Control
A Physical Security Team oversees implementation, audits and the annual review of the policy. The effectiveness of controls is continuously evaluated and adjusted according to emerging threats.
