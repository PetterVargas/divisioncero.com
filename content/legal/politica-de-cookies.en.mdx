At DivisionCero, we use cookies to improve your experience, keep the platform secure and offer you relevant features.

## Types of cookies we use

### Essential cookies

They enable basic functions such as signing in and session security. For example, the authentication cookies that keep you signed in.

### Performance cookies

They analyze usage patterns to improve the platform, such as tracking the pages you visit to optimize features.

### Functionality cookies

They store preferences to personalize your experience, such as the selected language.

### Marketing cookies (optional)

They deliver relevant content and measure campaign effectiveness through advertising aimed at returning users.

## Managing cookies

You can manage or disable cookies from your browser settings, although disabling essential cookies may limit the platform's functionality.

## Third-party cookies

We may work with trusted partners to provide analytics and marketing services.

## Your consent

Using the platform implies acceptance of our cookie practices. You can withdraw or change your consent at any time.

## Contact

If you have questions about our cookie policy, contact us through our official channels.
