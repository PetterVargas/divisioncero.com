---
title: "Auditing and Compliance of the Corporate Device Fleet"
description: "Verifying that policies are really followed"
---

Auditing and compliance of the corporate device fleet consists of periodically and systematically verifying that all the organization's devices really comply with the defined security policies, such as having up-to-date antivirus, active encryption and current patches, instead of assuming that the initially applied configurations remain intact over time. This matters in practice because configuration drift is common: a user may temporarily disable a control for convenience and forget to turn it back on, or a device may go weeks without connecting to the corporate network and fall out of date without anyone noticing. A concrete example is a compliance report revealing that a percentage of the company's laptops haven't applied a critical patch published a month ago, information that lets the security team prioritize following up on those specific devices before they become the entry point of an incident.
