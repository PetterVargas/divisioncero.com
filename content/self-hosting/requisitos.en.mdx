## Before installing

Make sure you have the following before you start the installation:

### Account with a supported provider

- **DigitalOcean**: to deploy a Droplet.
- **Hostinger**: to deploy a VPS.

Infrastructure costs are billed directly by the provider and **are not included** in DivisionCero plans.

### Domain

A domain or subdomain to access the platform (for example, `security.yourcompany.com`) and access to its DNS settings to point it to the server.

### Server access

- SSH access to the server with an SSH key (recommended over a password).
- A person on the team responsible for managing the server, its updates and backups.

### License

- **Community**: no purchase required; check that you meet the conditions of the [DivisionCero License](./licencia).
- **Paid plans**: buy the plan on the [pricing page](/en/precios?modelo=self-hosting) before installing.

### Cybersecurity best practices

- 🔐 Enable multi-factor authentication (MFA) on the provider account.
- 🧱 Expose only the necessary ports (SSH, HTTP and HTTPS).
- 💾 Enable the automatic backups the provider offers.
- 🔄 Keep the server's operating system up to date.
