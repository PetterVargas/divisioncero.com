## A win-win for CyberGuardians and DivisionCero

## Benefits

### Perpetual Commission

- 💰 **20% commission** on all sales to the referred customer.
- ♾️ **In perpetuity**: As long as the customer remains active and makes the corresponding payments, you receive a commission.
- 📈 **Recurring**: It applies to every annual renewal by the customer.

## Requirements and Clarifications

### CyberGuardian Requirements

To take part in the program you need to:

- ✅ **Be subscribed to DivisionCero's CyberGuardians model**
- ✅ **Give prior notice** of the purchase by the customer referred by the CyberGuardian.
- ✅ **Carry out an annual review** of the referred company's cybersecurity posture
- ✅ **Fill in the annual customer feedback form** with improvement proposals

### Purchase Conditions

The program applies under the following conditions:

- 📅 **Annual or longer purchases only**: It doesn't apply to monthly or quarterly plans
- 💳 **Commission payment**: Made **3 months after the customer's purchase**
- 📆 **Payment periods**: In **two specific months of the year** defined by DivisionCero

### Adjustments and Discounts

- 🏷️ **Customer discounts**: They also apply to the commission percentage
  - Example: If the customer has a 10% discount, the commission is calculated on the discounted value
- 💸 **Transaction fees**: Covered by the 20% commission
- 💳 **Payment method**: Defined by DivisionCero

### Conditions for Losing the Benefit

The commission is lost when:

- ⚠️ **Low NPS**: The customer gives a score of **6 or lower** on the Net Promoter Score (NPS)
- ❌ **Non-compliance**: The required annual review isn't carried out
- ❌ **Not filled in**: The annual feedback form

### Eligibility

- 👤 **Individuals only**: This program applies only to individuals
- 🤝 **Business partnerships**: If you represent a company, contact us for business partnership options

## Process for Referring Customers

1. **CyberGuardian subscription**: Activate #ApprenticeMode
2. **Benefits form**: Fill in the enrollment form for the CyberGuardian benefits program.
2. **Identify potential customers**: Companies that need cybersecurity services
3. **Make the referral**: Through the DivisionCero platform
4. **Support the customer**: Carry out the annual review of their cybersecurity posture
5. **Provide feedback**: Fill in the annual form with improvement proposals
6. **Receive your commission**: In the established payment periods

## CyberGuardian Responsibilities

As a certified referrer, your responsibilities include:

### Annual Cyber Posture Review

- 🔍 Assessment of the company's cybersecurity status
- 📊 Report of findings and recommendations
- 🎯 Identification of areas for improvement

### Annual Feedback Form

- 📝 The customer's experience with DivisionCero
- 💡 Service improvement proposals
- 📈 The customer's additional needs

### Maintaining NPS

- 😊 Ensure customer satisfaction (NPS > 6)
- 🤝 Be an additional point of contact
- 📞 Facilitate communication between the customer and DivisionCero

## Frequently Asked Questions

### When do I receive my first commission?

3 months after the customer makes their first annual purchase, in the payment month defined by DivisionCero.

### What happens if the customer renews?

You keep receiving the 20% commission on each renewal, as long as the program conditions are met.

### Can I refer multiple customers?

Yes, there's no limit on the number of customers you can refer.

### What happens if the customer cancels?

Commissions stop when the customer cancels their service. You only receive a commission for the time the customer remains active.

### How does a low NPS affect me?

If the customer gives a score of 6 or lower on the NPS, you lose the commission for that customer until the NPS is 7 or higher again.
