---
title: "Daily Alert Management and Prioritization"
description: "Prioritizing alerts in the SOC's daily operations"
---

In any SOC's daily operations, the number of alerts almost always exceeds the team's capacity to review them all in the same detail, and that's precisely why prioritization becomes a central discipline instead of a simple chronological order. Prioritizing well means combining the alert's technical severity with the value of the affected asset: a medium-severity alert on a server that processes customer data should be handled before a critical alert on a test machine with no sensitive data. In practice, many SOCs fall into the trap of handling alerts in the order they arrive or according to the vendor's default score, ignoring the business context only the team itself knows. Building a prioritization matrix that combines severity, asset criticality and the current threat context drastically reduces the risk of a serious incident getting lost in the noise of minor alerts.
