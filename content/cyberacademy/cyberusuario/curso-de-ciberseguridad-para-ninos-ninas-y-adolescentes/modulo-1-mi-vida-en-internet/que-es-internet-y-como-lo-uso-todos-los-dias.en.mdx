---
title: "What the Internet Is and How I Use It Every Day"
description: "What the internet is and how it's part of your day"
---

The internet is like a big network that connects computers, tablets and phones all over the world, letting you watch videos, play with friends who live far away, do school homework or video call your family, all from a screen. Even if you don't notice, you use the internet many times a day, from when you look up information for homework to when you watch your favorite show or play an online video game with other players. The internet itself isn't good or bad; it's a tool, a bit like a bicycle, that can take you to wonderful places if you use it carefully, but that also requires learning certain rules so you don't get hurt, like wearing a helmet when you ride a bike. Knowing how the internet works and what you use it for every day is the first step to learning how to move around it safely while enjoying all the good things it has to offer.
