---
title: "Relationship between Business Changes and Cyber Risk Exposure"
description: "How business changes expand cyber risk"
---

Every structural move an organization makes, whether opening a new market, bringing on a technology vendor or an internal reorganization, changes the attack surface available to an adversary. New systems, integrations and users connect to the environment before security controls finish adapting, and that time lag is precisely what attackers look to exploit. Understanding this relationship matters because it makes it possible to anticipate risk instead of reacting after an incident, involving security from the design of the change and not as a final formality. A common example is migrating a business area to a new SaaS platform, where temporary emergency access is enabled that's rarely revoked in time, leaving orphaned accounts with excessive privileges months after the project is completed.
