## What is this course for?

Risk management and security in the supply chain.

## What will I learn?

- 🚀 **Third-Party Risk Fundamentals**: Learn the cyber risk landscape in the supply chain.
- 🚀 **Vendor Assessment**: Assess and classify vendors according to their criticality and risk level.
- 🚀 **Contracting and Security Clauses**: Include security clauses and the right to audit in contracts.
- 🚀 **Continuous Third-Party Monitoring**: Continuously monitor your vendors' security posture.
- 🚀 **Third-Party Program Governance**: Report supply chain risk to senior management.

## Projects

With this course you'll create, optimize, automate or improve the following projects:

- 🚀 You'll implement third-party and supply chain risk assessment and management processes.
