## Purpose
Protect personal data, describe collection, processing and storage practices, and guarantee privacy rights and regulatory compliance.

## Scope
Applies to all of the organization's operations, including data collected directly from individuals and managed on behalf of customers, ensuring integrity, accuracy and alignment with organizational objectives.

## General Principles
- Collect only relevant, accurate, up-to-date and complete data.
- Transparency in the use and handling of personal data.
- Minimize the risk of unauthorized disclosure.
- Compliance with applicable data protection laws.

## Data Collection
- Data from interactions with the website, products and services.
- Automated data (IP, device) for analysis and improvements.
- Clear collection methods and notification to users.

## Use and Consent
- Data used only for the purposes explicitly communicated.
- Personal data isn't sold or shared without consent.
- For new purposes, explicit or implicit consent is obtained.
- Cookies and tracking technologies with user control.

## Security
- Technical and organizational controls to protect data in transit and at rest.
- Protocols against unauthorized access, corruption or loss of data.
- Assessment and monitoring of third parties that process data.

## Third Parties and Disclosure
- Disclosure only with consent or by legal mandate.
- Agreements with third parties include robust privacy commitments.
- Recording of all disclosures to maintain transparency.

## Data Breaches
- Clear notification procedures for data subjects and regulators.
- Action plans to remediate and prevent future incidents.

## Data Subject Rights
- Access, rectification, deletion and inquiry regarding their data.
- Direct communication with the organization or regulatory bodies.
- Detailed reports on their data and the disclosures made.

## Monitoring and Review
- Regular policy updates with prior notice to users.
- Ongoing evaluation of privacy practices for compliance with standards and best practices.
- Processes for privacy inquiries, complaints and dispute resolution.
