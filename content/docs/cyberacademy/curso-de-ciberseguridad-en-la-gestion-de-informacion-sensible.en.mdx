## What is this course for?

Well, team, we constantly work with the crown jewel without realizing it: that information we see as trivial in a spreadsheet, in a file on the desktop, may be considered the holy grail by attackers. Let's take care of it.

## What will I learn?

- 🚀 **Sensitive Information Fundamentals**: Learn to classify sensitive and confidential information.
- 🚀 **Secure Handling and Storage**: Store and encrypt sensitive information securely.
- 🚀 **Transmitting and Sharing Information**: Share sensitive information only through approved channels.
- 🚀 **Lifecycle and Retention**: Learn the policies for retaining and securely destroying information.
- 🚀 **Compliance and Incident Response**: Respond appropriately to a data leak or exposure.
