## What is this course for?

Designing and implementing BCP and DRP plans for cyber incidents.

## What will I learn?

- 🚀 **Business Continuity Fundamentals**: Learn the differences between BCP, DRP and crisis management, and their regulatory framework.
- 🚀 **Business Impact Analysis (BIA)**: Carry out a Business Impact Analysis and define RTO and RPO for each critical process.
- 🚀 **Designing the Continuity Plan**: Design continuity strategies and alternate backup sites.
- 🚀 **Disaster Recovery Plan (DRP)**: Define recovery strategies for critical infrastructure and data.
- 🚀 **Testing and Maintaining the Plan**: Run drills and keep the continuity plan up to date.

## Projects

With this course you'll create, optimize, automate or improve the following projects:

- 🚀 You'll develop business continuity plans (BCP) with disaster recovery procedures.
- 🚀 You'll implement automated backup systems and high availability strategies.
