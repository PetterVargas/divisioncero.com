### Free use (Community)

You can use the Community edition at no cost when your organization meets **all** of these conditions:

- ✅ It has **up to 9 employees**.
- ✅ Its annual revenue is **equal to or below 100K USD**.

### Commercial use (paid plans)

You need a [paid plan](./planes) when your organization:

- 👥 Exceeds the Community edition's number of employees.
- 💵 Has annual revenue above 100K USD, regardless of its number of employees.
- 🔑 Requires exclusive features, such as Single Sign-On (SSO).

The paid license is granted **per organization**, according to the purchased plan, and remains valid while the plan is active and payments are up to date.
