SOC 2 is an audit standard developed by the AICPA for service providers that store customer data in the cloud. It's based on the Trust Services Criteria (TSC), organized into 5 categories: Security (mandatory) and four optional ones. Kudo maps its criteria to the framework and the ISMS policies.
