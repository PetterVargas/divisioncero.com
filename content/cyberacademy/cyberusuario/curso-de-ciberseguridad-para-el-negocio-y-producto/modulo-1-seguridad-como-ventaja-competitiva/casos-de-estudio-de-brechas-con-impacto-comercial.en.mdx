---
title: "Case Studies of Breaches with Business Impact"
description: "Real examples of breaches that affected the business"
---

Analyzing real cases of companies that suffered security breaches helps you understand that the business impact is almost never limited to the technical cost of resolving the incident, but includes lost customers, a falling share price, regulatory fines and even the cancellation of contracts with partners that required certain security standards. Studying these cases in detail, instead of just remembering the headline, makes it possible to identify patterns that repeat again and again, such as the lack of a prepared communication plan or the delay in notifying affected users, mistakes that worsened the damage more than the technical attack itself. For a business team, reviewing these examples provides a concrete, evidence-based argument for justifying security investment, showing with real figures how much it can cost not to have prioritized it in time.
