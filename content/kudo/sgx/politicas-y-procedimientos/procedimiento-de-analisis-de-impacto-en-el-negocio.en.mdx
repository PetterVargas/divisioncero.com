---
title: "Business Impact Analysis (BIA) Procedure"
description: "Methodology for business continuity impact analysis (BIA)"
---

## Purpose
Determine critical processes, prioritize their recovery after disruptions and define the necessary resources, considering internal and external dependencies.

## Scope
Applies to all of the organization's business processes and facilities.

## Objectives
- Identify critical processes and systems.
- Assess the impact of disruptions.
- Define recovery requirements and windows.
- Identify dependencies.
- Prioritize business functions.

## Key Definitions
- **BIA:** Analysis of functions and the effects of disruptions.
- **MBCO:** Minimum acceptable level of services during a disruption.
- **MTPD:** Maximum tolerable time before unacceptable impacts.
- **RPO:** Point to which data must be restored.
- **RTO:** Maximum time to resume a service or process.
- **SME:** Subject matter experts.

## Responsibilities
- **Management:** Review the BIA, assess losses and determine recovery times.
- **BCM:** Plan workshops, gather information, analyze data, assess risks and develop recovery plans.

## Training Standards
- Formal annual training on risk, incidents and compliance.
- Periodic awareness campaigns.
- Security training during staff onboarding.
- Attendance records and refresher sessions.

## Procedure
1. **Information Gathering:**
   Interviews, questionnaires and workshops to describe business services and activities.

2. **Assessment and Analysis:**
   - Assess the impact of disruptions using BIA criteria.
   - Rate economic, social, legal, technological and environmental impacts.
   - Assess the time scale: 4 hours to 30 days.
   - Document assumptions and ratings to develop recovery strategies.

3. **Defining Critical Objectives:**
   - **RPO:** Data restoration point.
   - **RTO:** Maximum time to resume services.
   - **MBCO:** Minimum acceptable level of continuity.
   - **MTPD:** Maximum tolerable disruption time.

4. **Prioritizing Functions:**
   Determine critical processes and dependencies to guide recovery.

## Monitoring and Review
- Regular reviews to assess the effectiveness of the BIA.
- Periodic reports to management.
- Updates based on operational or environmental changes.
