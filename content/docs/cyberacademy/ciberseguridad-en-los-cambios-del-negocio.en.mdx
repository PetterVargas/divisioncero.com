## What is this course for?

Secure management of organizational and technological changes.

## What will I learn?

- 🚀 **Managing Risk in Organizational Change**: Understand the relationship between business changes and cyber risk.
- 🚀 **Security in Mergers and Acquisitions**: Assess security posture in merger and acquisition processes.
- 🚀 **Technology Change Management**: Securely manage technology change and its approvals.
- 🚀 **Security Continuity during Transformation**: Maintain security controls and access management during the transition.
- 🚀 **Change Governance and Continuous Improvement**: Establish a secure change committee and apply lessons learned.

## Projects

With this course you'll create, optimize, automate or improve the following projects:

- 🚀 You'll configure change management processes with impact analysis and automatic rollback.
