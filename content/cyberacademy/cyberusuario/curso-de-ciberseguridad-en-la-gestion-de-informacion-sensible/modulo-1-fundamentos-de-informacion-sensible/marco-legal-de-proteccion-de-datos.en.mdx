---
title: "Legal Framework for Data Protection"
description: "Which laws protect personal information"
---

Most countries today have personal data protection laws that define how organizations must collect, store and use people's information, establishing rights such as knowing what data they hold about you, correcting it or asking for it to be deleted. Although these laws sound like something only lawyers deal with, they actually directly affect your daily work, because every time you handle customer, employee or user data, you're subject to those legal obligations regardless of whether your job is technical. Failing to comply with this legal framework can bring significant financial penalties for the organization, and in many cases individual liability for the person who handled the data improperly, even without intending to cause harm. A nuance that often surprises people is that these laws also apply to data that seems minor, such as a contact email list stored without authorization, so understanding the basic principles of this legal framework helps avoid mistakes that seemed harmless.
