## What is this course for?

Playbooks, containment, eradication and lessons learned.

## What will I learn?

- 🚀 **Incident Response Fundamentals**: Learn the NIST/SANS frameworks and the roles of the response team.
- 🚀 **Preparation and Detection**: Write runbooks and detect incidents early.
- 🚀 **Containment, Eradication and Recovery**: Apply containment, eradication and secure recovery strategies.
- 🚀 **Communication and Coordination**: Coordinate internal, legal and authority communication during an incident.
- 🚀 **Lessons Learned and Continuous Improvement**: Write the post-incident report and update plans with what was learned.

## Projects

With this course you'll create, optimize, automate or improve the following projects:

- 🚀 You'll develop incident response playbooks with digital forensics and threat intelligence.
