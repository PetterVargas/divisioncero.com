---
title: "Physical and Digital Storage Best Practices"
description: "How to store sensitive information correctly"
---

Storing sensitive information securely doesn't depend only on technology tools, but also on simple physical habits that are sometimes overlooked, such as not leaving important documents on the desk when leaving the office or keeping folders with confidential data under lock and key when they're not in use. In the digital realm, this translates into organizing information in folders with access restricted according to who really needs it, instead of storing it in generally shared locations just for convenience. A frequent mistake is mixing sensitive information with personal files or unrelated projects, which makes it harder to apply consistent access controls and increases the chance of sharing it by mistake along with other files. Establishing a clear structure from the start, both on the physical desk and in digital folders, where each type of information has a defined place and a corresponding access level, makes it much easier to protect it consistently day to day.
