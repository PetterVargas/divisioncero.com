---
title: "Evidence and Documentation for Continuous Compliance"
description: "Keeping compliance evidence always current"
---

In a continuous compliance model, evidence isn't generated once a year for the audit; it's collected and updated constantly as a natural byproduct of operations, for example automatic snapshots of security configurations, records of change approvals or periodic exports of access review reports, stored centrally with a verifiable timestamp. This ongoing documentation has a key practical advantage over the traditional approach, because it makes it possible to answer an auditor's question about any specific date of the year with real evidence, not only about the moment the audit was prepared. The important nuance is that accumulating evidence without organization becomes as useless as not having it, so a clear structure is needed that links each piece of evidence to the control and regulatory requirement it supports, along with a retention policy that defines how long each type of document must be kept according to the applicable legal requirements.
