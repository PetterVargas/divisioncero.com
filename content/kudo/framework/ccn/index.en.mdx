## Domain Description

Domain focused on **organizational resilience, operational continuity and controlled change management 
in the business environment**. It covers *change management and control procedures, quality and 
security testing before deployments, and the ability to revert changes when they fail (rollback).* It promotes the *identification 
of single points of failure*, the use of the *shared responsibility model with cloud providers (CSPs)* and the 
implementation of *deployment strategies such as blue/green*. 
Its purpose is to *ensure that technology changes don't compromise service availability or the 
customer experience*.
