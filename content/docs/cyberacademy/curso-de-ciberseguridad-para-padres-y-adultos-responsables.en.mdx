## What is this course for?

This space is for anyone interested in protecting, accompanying and guiding children and teenagers in safer digital environments.

## What will I learn?

- 🚀 **The Digital World of Children and Teenagers**: Learn about the platforms and digital risks according to children's ages.
- 🚀 **Specific Risks and Threats**: Identify grooming, cyberbullying and other risks for children.
- 🚀 **Protection Tools**: Set up parental controls and healthy screen time limits.
- 🚀 **Communication and Support**: Learn to talk about digital safety and build trust with your children.
- 🚀 **Acting in the Event of an Incident**: Learn the steps to take in cases of cyberbullying, grooming or another incident.

## Projects

With this course you'll create, optimize, automate or improve the following projects:

- 🚀 You'll set up parental controls on devices, apps and social networks.
- 🚀 You'll establish rules for responsible use and healthy screen time.
- 🚀 You'll identify and prevent risks such as cyberbullying, grooming and sextortion.
- 🚀 You'll configure privacy on platforms children use (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube).
- 🚀 You'll create open communication strategies about family digital safety.
- 🚀 You'll implement filters for inappropriate content and safe browsing.
- 🚀 You'll teach about digital footprint and online reputation from an early age.
- 🚀 You'll develop action protocols for digital risk situations.
