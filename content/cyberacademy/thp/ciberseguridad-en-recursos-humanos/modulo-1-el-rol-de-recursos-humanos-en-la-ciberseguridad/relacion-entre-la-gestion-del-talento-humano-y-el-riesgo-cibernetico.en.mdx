---
title: "The Relationship between Human Talent Management and Cyber Risk"
description: "How HR influences cyber risk"
---

Human Resources is often seen as an area removed from cybersecurity, but in practice it directly manages several of the decisions that most influence an organization's cyber risk: who gets hired, what information they're entrusted with from day one, and when and how that access is withdrawn when they leave. A hiring process without proper verification can bring someone with malicious intentions into a sensitive role, while a slow offboarding process can leave a disgruntled former employee's account active. Recognizing this relationship lets HR stop seeing security as a purely technical issue and start incorporating simple controls, such as timely coordination with IT, into its own usual people management processes.
