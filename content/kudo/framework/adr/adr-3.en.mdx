---
title: ADR-3 - Log Retention and Monitoring
description: Control for log management and security event monitoring
---

## Control Definition

Activity logs from systems, applications and infrastructure relevant to information security must be generated, collected and centralized in a SIEM, ensuring their complete transmission and availability during the defined retention periods. Logs must be protected against alteration or deletion and used to detect and alert on anomalous behavior in a timely manner through continuous monitoring by the security team.

## Requirements

### Requirement 1

We collect and centralize security logs from all the organization's systems, applications and infrastructure in a SIEM.

### Requirement 2

We define and cover the mandatory log sources: operating systems, critical applications, firewalls, proxies, identity providers and cloud systems.

### Requirement 3

We stream logs to the SIEM in real time and periodically validate the integrity of the data flow.

### Requirement 4

We retain logs in active storage in the SIEM for a defined time.

### Requirement 5

We retain logs in archive storage for a defined period of time.

### Requirement 6

We protect archived logs against modification through immutability controls (WORM storage).

### Requirement 7

We implement correlation and detection rules in the SIEM to identify anomalous behavior of high interest.

### Requirement 8

We alert automatically on: multiple authentication failures, out-of-hours access to critical systems, data exfiltration, lateral movement and privilege escalation.

### Requirement 9

We enable and configure the logging of activities, errors, failures and other events relevant to information security on all source systems.

### Requirement 10

We protect logs in active storage and in transit against alteration or deletion, complementing the immutability controls applied to archive storage.

### Requirement 11

We inventory log sources and review their coverage on a scheduled basis.

### Requirement 12

The SOC team operates continuous security monitoring dashboards.
