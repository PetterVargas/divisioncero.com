The Cloud Security Alliance AI Control Matrix (CSA AICM) is a security control framework designed specifically for artificial intelligence and machine learning systems. It complements the CSA CCM by extending coverage to the complete lifecycle of AI models. Kudo maps its domains and controls to the framework and the ISMS policies.
