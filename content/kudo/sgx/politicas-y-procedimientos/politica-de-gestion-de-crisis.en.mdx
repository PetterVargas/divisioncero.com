---
title: "Crisis Management Policy"
description: "Framework for responding to and managing organizational crisis situations"
---

## Purpose
Establish standards and procedures for crisis communication, ensuring an effective, coordinated response to disruptions affecting critical operations, people, assets or reputation.

## Objectives
- Systematic, consistent communication during crises.
- Crisis identification, communication management and documentation of lessons learned.
- Keep internal and external interested parties informed.
- Notify customers and regulators according to legal and contractual obligations.
- Share relevant crisis information with industry partners and authorities when appropriate.

## Scope
Applies to all crises within the organization and to all communication phases, from identification to post-event review.

## Definitions
- **Crisis:** A significant disruption that affects operations and creates potential legal, financial, political or media impacts.
- **Crisis Management Team (CMT):** Senior management responsible for the corporate response.
- **Crisis Communication Team (CCT):** Manages internal and external communication about the crisis.

## Roles and Responsibilities
- **Crisis Team Lead/Spokesperson:** Strategic decisions and public statements.
- **Crisis Communication Lead:** Coordinates internal and external communication and represents the organization.
- **Human Resources:** Employee safety and accountability.
- **Legal and Regulatory:** Review and approval of messages.
- **Business Recovery Lead:** Manages business impacts and BCM.
- **Commercial/Financial Lead:** Manages financial and strategic aspects.
- **Safety and Recovery:** On-site safety procedures.
- **IT:** Technology support and communication channels.

## Crisis Response Plan (CRP)
- Defines key interested parties, roles, responsibilities and steps to contain, investigate and remediate the crisis.
- Categorization of crises by severity and impact to prioritize resources.
- Documentation and recording of all crises.

## Crisis Communication Process
1. **Alert:** Notification to the Communication Lead within 15 min; the CCT is activated.
2. **Assessment:** Information gathering and incident classification.
3. **Activation:** Contact with affected areas, internal updates, and development and approval of messages by key leaders.
4. **Post-Mortem:** Review of the response, lessons learned and update of the CRP.

## Escalation
A clear escalation path with defined criteria and owners for major crises.

## Coordination and Information Sharing
- Procedures for coordinating with external parties (authorities, industry partners).
- Voluntary, secure information sharing, respecting privacy and regulations.
- Clear roles for external communication.

## Lessons Learned
- Root cause analysis and corrective actions to prevent recurrence.
- Update of the CRP based on findings.

## Documentation and Notification
- All phases documented and closed with the Communication Lead's approval.
- Prompt notification to customers and regulators according to legal and contractual obligations.

## Testing and Training
- Regular exercises, including scenarios with external parties, to maintain preparedness and effectiveness.

## Monitoring and Review
- Annual review by the CCT and management to ensure compliance with the Information Security Management System.
- Adjustments based on lessons learned and best practices.
