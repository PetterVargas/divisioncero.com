---
title: "Communications Security Policy"
description: "Protection of communications and information transfers"
---

## Purpose
Ensure the protection of information during its transmission and transfer, both internally and externally.

## Scope
Applies to all communications and data transfers, including email, files, mobile devices and messaging platforms.

## Standards

- **Encryption:** All transfers must use recognized encryption techniques, validated by the information security team.
- **Passwords:** Encrypted transfers must comply with the company's password policies.
- **Information Classification:** Information must be classified according to internal policies to ensure adequate protection during transfer.
- **Email and Insecure Channels:** Transmitting sensitive information through insecure means is prohibited; if email is used, encryption and a password are required.
- **Mobile Devices:** Only corporate devices with encryption and password protection should be used; the use of personal devices is discouraged.
- **File Transfer:** Use secure protocols such as SFTP or VPN for sensitive data.
- **Monitoring and Review:** Regular assessments to ensure compliance and continuous improvement.

## Monitoring and Action

- **Oversight:** The information security team, together with IT, monitors communication and transfer practices.
- **Incidents:** Any non-compliance is reported immediately; it's investigated and corrected with appropriate measures.
