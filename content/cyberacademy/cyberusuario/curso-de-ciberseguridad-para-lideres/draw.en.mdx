The Cybersecurity for Leaders Course is part of the CyberUsuario learning path; we wrote this description to give you a 
mind map of what we're achieving and how everything is part of an ecosystem focused on continuous improvement. 

And as leaders we have a moral obligation to be empathetic about cybersecurity in what we lead: which roles and responsibilities 
are needed for this approach, from making sure an employee who works with us has the necessary tools to decisions 
that can determine whether an entire business survives or not as a result of a critical cybersecurity incident.
