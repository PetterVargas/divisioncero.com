## Domain Description

It establishes the foundation for **cybersecurity governance, strategy and compliance**, ensuring that the documented policies, 
procedures and controls **reflect the strategic and operational reality and the agreements reached 
with stakeholders**. It covers *organizational risk management*, *regulatory compliance mapping*, 
*independent risk-based assessment* and the mechanisms for *continuous improvement and corrective actions*. 

Its central goal is to *align business objectives with the cybersecurity strategy through effective governance 
structures, defined roles, and periodic review and audit cycles.*
