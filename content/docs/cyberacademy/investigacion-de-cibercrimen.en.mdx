## What is this course for?

Investigation and analysis techniques for computer crimes.

## What will I learn?

- 🚀 **The Cybercrime Landscape**: Learn the typologies, actors and underground economy of cybercrime.
- 🚀 **Legal Framework for Cybercrime Investigation**: Understand the applicable legislation and international cooperation on cybercrime.
- 🚀 **Digital Investigation Techniques**: Trace cryptocurrency transactions and analyze attacker infrastructure.
- 🚀 **Digital Evidence in Criminal Proceedings**: Preserve digital evidence that's valid for judicial proceedings and expert examinations.
- 🚀 **Coordination with Authorities**: Coordinate reporting to authorities and collaboration with national CERTs.
