---
title: "Visual and Audio Privacy at Home"
description: "Taking care of what's seen and heard on work calls"
---

When you work from home, your personal surroundings unintentionally become the visible background of your video calls and the sound environment of your work phone conversations, which can expose information without you realizing it. A whiteboard with internal notes, an open document on the desk or even a family photo with identifiable details in the background can attract the wrong attention during a meeting with external clients. Likewise, talking about confidential topics out loud near open windows or in shared spaces of the home risks someone overhearing information that shouldn't leave that context. Using virtual backgrounds, choosing a fixed spot for work calls and letting the people you live with know when you're in an important meeting are simple adjustments that make a real difference. Taking care of this kind of privacy isn't exaggerating; it's recognizing that the home office is still, for practical purposes, a workspace.
