description: "Detection, response and management of security incidents"
---

## Purpose
Establish standards and procedures for the systematic management of incidents, aligned with the Information Security Management System, minimizing legal, contractual and reputational impacts.

## Objectives
- Record and document all incidents consistently.
- Identify, contain and learn from incidents.
- Keep internal and external interested parties informed.
- Document and close analyses with management approval.
- Promptly notify affected customers and regulators.
- Share relevant information with third parties to strengthen cybersecurity situational awareness.

## Scope
Applies to all internal incidents, from identification to post-incident review.

## Key Definitions
- **Incident:** An event that causes a service disruption and requires a managed response.
- **Crisis:** A significant disruption with potential political, legal, financial or reputational impacts.
- **Emergency:** An unplanned event with a risk of serious harm, deaths or critical disruption of operations.
- **IMT (Incident Management Team):** Team that manages the incident response in its area or facility.
- **BCMS:** Business continuity management system.

## Roles and Responsibilities
- **Incident Commander:** Leads the overall management of the incident.
- **Incident Management Team (IMT):** Coordinates tactical actions, containment, communication and recovery.
- **Communications Officer:** Manages internal and external communication.
- **HR, Logistics, Operations and Security Coordinators:** Support with staff, infrastructure, operational continuity and security.
- **BCMS Representative and Risk Officer:** Risk assessment and coordination of solutions.

## Incident Management Process
1. **Notification and Assessment:** Record the incident and assess its severity and potential impacts.
2. **IMT Activation:** Notify and mobilize resources according to the incident level.
3. **Developing Recommendations:** Identify containment and recovery actions.
4. **Communication:** Inform internal and external stakeholders clearly and in a timely manner.
5. **Post-Incident Action:** Declare closure, analyze causes, document lessons learned and update processes.

## Incident Classification
- **Level 1 (Minor):** Limited local impact.
- **Level 2 (Moderate):** Moderate local impact, visible to the customer.
- **Level 3 (Significant):** Large-scale incident affecting multiple departments.
- **Level 4 (Catastrophic):** Critical long-term impact on the business.

## Incident Response Plan (IRP)
- Defines roles, responsibilities and steps to contain, investigate and remediate incidents.
- Establishes protocols for coordination with external interested parties and the voluntary sharing of cybersecurity information.
- Covers containment, escalation, notification and structured reporting.

## Documentation and Notification
- Record all phases of the incident with the approval of management and the security representative.
- Ensure prompt notification to customers, regulators and relevant third parties.

## Testing and Training
- Run simulations, tabletop tests and regular exercises to keep the IRP effective.
- Include coordination with external parties (agencies, industry peers).

## Continuous Improvement
- Review and update the program based on incidents, test results and training.
- Ensure alignment with best practices and the requirements of the Information Security Management System.

## Monitoring and Review
- Annual review of the program and continuity plans to ensure accuracy, effectiveness and preparedness.
- Validate communication and coordination protocols with internal and external stakeholders.
