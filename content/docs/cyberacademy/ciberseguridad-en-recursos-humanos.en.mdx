## What is this course for?

Training, secure onboarding and a security culture for human talent.

## What will I learn?

- 🚀 **The Role of Human Resources in Cybersecurity**: Understand the relationship between human talent management and cyber risk.
- 🚀 **Security in the Hiring Process**: Run background checks and include security clauses in contracts.
- 🚀 **Access Management during Employment**: Provision access by role and continuously train staff.
- 🚀 **Offboarding Management**: Deactivate access and recover assets when employment ends.
- 🚀 **Security Culture from Human Resources**: Integrate security into the organizational culture from Human Resources.

## Projects

With this course you'll create, optimize, automate or improve the following projects:

- 🚀 You'll develop cybersecurity awareness programs with phishing simulations and interactive training.
- 🚀 You'll configure automated onboarding/offboarding systems with role-based access controls.
