## Description

Hi team, good day, and welcome to this course we've built for anyone taking their first step into the wonderful world of
Cybersecurity. You don't need any prior knowledge, just be in **#EagerToLearn** mode and be aware that cybersecurity is 
everyone's job: however trivial we think a situation is, we can protect many people.
