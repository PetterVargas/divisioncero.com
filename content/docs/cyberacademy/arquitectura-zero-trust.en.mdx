## What is this course for?

Zero Trust principles, design and implementation.

## What will I learn?

- 🚀 **Zero Trust Fundamentals**: Learn the principles and pillars of a Zero Trust architecture.
- 🚀 **Identity as the New Perimeter**: Apply continuous identity verification and adaptive authentication.
- 🚀 **Segmentation and Microsegmentation**: Reduce the attack surface with network microsegmentation.
- 🚀 **Implementing Zero Trust**: Draw up your adoption roadmap with technologies such as ZTNA, SASE and CASB.
- 🚀 **Monitoring and Continuous Improvement**: Continuously monitor policies and evolve the model in the face of new threats.

## Projects

With this course you'll create, optimize, automate or improve the following projects:

- 🚀 You'll configure Zero Trust policies for access to corporate resources.
