---
title: "Secure Change Management in Technology Infrastructure"
description: "A framework for infrastructure changes"
---

Managing change in technology infrastructure securely means applying a consistent framework (impact assessment, maintenance window, rollback plan and subsequent validation) to every relevant change to servers, networks or cloud services, regardless of the urgency perceived by whoever requests it. The discipline here lies in resisting the temptation to skip steps when business pressure is high, because that's precisely when the likelihood of introducing a vulnerability or a service outage increases. Having a tested rollback plan, and not just a documented one, is what really reduces recovery time when something goes wrong. An illustrative example is updating an identity management system, where skipping the rollback plan can leave the whole organization without access for hours if the new configuration fails in production.
