## What is this course for?

Methodologies for risk analysis, assessment and treatment.

## What will I learn?

- 🚀 **Risk Management Fundamentals**: Learn the key concepts of risk and organizational risk appetite.
- 🚀 **Risk Identification and Assessment**: Identify and assess cyber risks with qualitative and quantitative matrices.
- 🚀 **Risk Treatment**: Design action plans to mitigate, transfer or accept risks.
- 🚀 **Third-Party Risk Management**: Assess and monitor the cyber risk of vendors and third parties.
- 🚀 **Risk Governance**: Record and report organizational risk to committees and senior management.

## Projects

With this course you'll create, optimize, automate or improve the following projects:

- 🚀 Cybersecurity Risk Management.
