---
title: "Information Security Policy"
description: "Protection of the organization's information, assets and reputation"
---

## Purpose
Establish standards and guidelines to protect the organization's information, assets and reputation, ensuring regulatory and contractual compliance and good security practices.

## Scope
Applies to all people, processes and technologies involved in the development, maintenance and delivery of the organization's software and technology services (Website, SaaS, Consulting, Support).

## Leadership and Governance
- Management actively supports information security, defines roles and responsibilities and ensures adequate resources and training.
- The Board of Directors independently oversees controls and policies, including the involvement of external advisors if necessary.
- All staff and interested parties must comply with the policy; violations lead to proportionate disciplinary action.

## Roles and Responsibilities
- Clear, documented roles in: Management, Information Security, IT, Finance, HR and Development.
- Segregation of duties to ensure independence in critical activities.
- Ongoing training and communication of security expectations according to each role.

## Compliance and Contracts
- All employees and contractors must sign confidentiality and intellectual property clauses.
- Security responsibilities that remain in force after termination or change of employment are formally defined and communicated.
- Ongoing information security education and training is ensured.

## Risk Management and Cybersecurity
- Identify, assess, treat and monitor information risks.
- Integrate cybersecurity risk management into strategic, operational and project decision-making.
- Document risks, mitigation measures and residual risk.
- Periodically review policies, controls and processes in light of legal and regulatory changes and emerging threats.

## ISMS Operation (Information Security Management System)
- Maintain documented, up-to-date and accessible policies, standards and procedures.
- Train staff and assess competencies so they can fulfill their roles.
- Document exceptions and deviations with approval from Management and the Security Team.
- Periodically review control activities and adjust them to the needs of the security environment.

## Operating Procedures
- Document procedures for critical, new or infrequent activities.
- Specify owners, configurations, information handling, backup, monitoring, maintenance and support contacts.
- Review and update procedures when there are significant changes.

## Contact with Authorities and Interested Parties
- Inform Management and authorities of incidents that violate legal, regulatory or contractual rules.
- Maintain regular communication about information security with customers, auditors, partners, the media and internal staff.
- Consider the impact of third parties on structures, roles and responsibilities.

## Legislation and Regulations
- Comply with GDPR, CCPA and equivalent local data protection laws.
- Identify and comply with industry-specific regulations.
- Incorporate legal and regulatory changes into the ISMS to minimize legal risks.

## Reporting and Confidential Channels
- Periodic meetings to review risks and control performance and update documents.
- Confidential channels for reporting security violations or problems, guaranteeing anonymity or confidentiality.

## Monitoring and Review
- Internal audits and periodic reviews of the ISMS to ensure compliance, effectiveness and continuous improvement.
- Identify areas for improvement and implement corrective and preventive actions.
- Management receives regular updates on performance and corrective actions to ensure the ongoing effectiveness of the ISMS.

## Continuous Improvement
- Regularly evaluate the effectiveness of the ISMS and make adjustments to improve information security.
- Document findings, actions and results for organizational learning and traceability.
