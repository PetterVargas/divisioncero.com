## What is this course for?

Hi team, good day, and welcome to this course we've built for remote work, which grew exponentially after the pandemic, and for everything related to working with mobile devices.

## What will I learn?

- 🚀 **Secure Remote Work Fundamentals**: Learn the risks of teleworking and your responsibilities as a remote employee.
- 🚀 **Home and Network Security**: Configure your home network and use the corporate VPN securely.
- 🚀 **Devices and Access**: Protect your devices and manage your remote access securely.
- 🚀 **Workspaces and Video Calls**: Protect your visual and audio privacy and your privacy in video conferences.
- 🚀 **Secure Remote Collaboration**: Collaborate and share files securely while out of the office.
- 🚀 **Mobile Security Fundamentals**: Learn the risks specific to smartphones and tablets.
- 🚀 **Apps and Permissions**: Download apps safely and manage their permissions.
- 🚀 **Connectivity and Networks**: Protect your connection when using public Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and mobile data.
- 🚀 **Protecting Data on the Device**: Encrypt, back up and protect the information on your device.
- 🚀 **Responsible Use and Privacy**: Configure your privacy and separate personal from corporate use.

## Projects

With this course you'll create, optimize, automate or improve the following projects:

- 🚀 You'll configure privacy on social networks and mobile devices.
- 🚀 You'll protect your home Wi-Fi network with secure settings.
