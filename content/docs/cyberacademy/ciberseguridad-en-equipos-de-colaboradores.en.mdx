## What is this course for?

EDR, device management and protection of work devices.

## What will I learn?

- 🚀 **Workstation Security**: Apply hardening and security policies to corporate devices.
- 🚀 **Endpoint Threat Protection**: Protect endpoints with EDR/XDR and control external devices.
- 🚀 **Corporate Device and BYOD Management**: Manage corporate and personal devices with UEM platforms.
- 🚀 **Endpoint Encryption and Data Protection**: Encrypt disks and prevent data leaks from employees' devices.
- 🚀 **Device Lifecycle Management**: Provision and decommission devices securely and auditably.

## Projects

With this course you'll create, optimize, automate or improve the following projects:

- 🚀 You'll deploy endpoint protection solutions with automated detection and response.
