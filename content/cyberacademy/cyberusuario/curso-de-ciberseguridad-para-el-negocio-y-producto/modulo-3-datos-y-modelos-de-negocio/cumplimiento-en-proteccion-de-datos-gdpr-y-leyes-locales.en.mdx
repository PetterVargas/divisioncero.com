---
title: "Data Protection Compliance (GDPR and Local Laws)"
description: "Legal requirements for handling personal data"
---

Complying with regulations such as the European GDPR or local data protection laws isn't just a legal issue, but a condition for being able to operate in many markets and avoid fines that can reach millions of dollars depending on the company's revenue. These rules require concrete things such as obtaining clear consent to use data, allowing users to delete it if they request it, and notifying authorities within strict deadlines when a leak occurs, usually seventy-two hours. For a product team, this translates into practical design decisions, such as not enabling location tracking by default or including a visible button for users to download or delete their information without having to write to support and wait for days.
