---
title: "My Personal Information Is a Treasure"
description: "Your personal information is worth a lot, take care of it"
---

Your full name, your address, the school you go to, your phone number and even photos you appear in: all of that is personal information, and it's like a treasure that only you and your family should decide whom to show. When you're on the internet, playing or chatting, sometimes someone may ask you for that information while pretending to be a friendly person, but just as you wouldn't give the key to your house to a stranger on the street, you shouldn't give your personal information to someone you only know through a screen. For example, if a game asks for your address to "send you a prize", that's a warning sign, because serious game companies almost never need that information for you to keep playing. Taking care of your treasure means thinking twice before sharing it and, if you're not sure, always asking a trusted adult before typing any of your details.
