---
title: "Cloud Services Policy"
description: "Secure use and management of cloud and SaaS services"
---

## Purpose
Establish guidelines for the secure use of cloud services, ensuring the protection and management of sensitive information in line with the organization's information security program.

## Scope
Applies to all employees, contractors and third parties who use cloud services (IaaS, PaaS, SaaS) within the organization's environment.

## Objectives
- Ensure the secure use of cloud services.
- Comply with the information security management program.
- Guide employees on appropriate use.
- Protect the confidentiality, integrity and availability of data in the cloud.

## Standards

**Approval and Contracts**
- Every cloud service must be approved by IT, Information Security and management.
- Review and approve contracts before use, including security and confidentiality clauses.

**Encryption and Key Management**
- All data in the cloud must be encrypted with approved algorithms.
- Manage cryptographic keys securely and restrict access to authorized staff.

**Patches and Updates**
- Keep all cloud services up to date and regularly patched.

**Access Control**
- Restrict access to authorized staff only.
- Use multi-factor authentication (MFA) whenever possible.

**Incident Management**
- Immediately report incidents or breaches to IT and Information Security.
- Follow incident response plans.

**Data Retention and Deletion**
- Delete sensitive data when it's no longer needed, following internal policies and applicable regulations.
- Document evidence of deletion; require confirmation from external providers where applicable.

**Capacity Management**
- Monitor and test the capacity of cloud services to ensure availability and performance.
- Consider future projections and avoid dependence on key resources.

**Monitoring and Review**
- Regularly review and audit the use of cloud services to ensure compliance with this policy and the security program.

## Policy Review
IT and Information Security will periodically review and update this policy to keep it current and aligned with the organization's needs.
