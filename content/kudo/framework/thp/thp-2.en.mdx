---
title: THP-2 - Remote Work
description: Security control for employees working remotely or in hybrid mode
---

## Control Definition

Security controls must be established to ensure remote employees have the same level of protection as in the physical facilities, requiring a secure connection and multi-factor authentication to access corporate resources. Remote work devices must be managed through centralized solutions that enforce encryption, locking and usage restrictions, and only collaboration tools with a secure corporate configuration must be approved. Compliance with these policies must be assessed periodically, and information accessed, processed or stored outside the organization's facilities must be protected.

## Requirements

### Requirement 1

We establish security controls to ensure remote employees maintain the same level of protection as in the physical facilities.

### Requirement 2

We evaluate and approve officially used cloud collaboration tools with a secure corporate configuration.

### Requirement 3

We prohibit the use of public Wi-Fi networks without an active VPN.

### Requirement 4

We communicate and enforce the clean desk policy in home environments.

### Requirement 5

We apply security measures to protect information accessed when staff work remotely.

### Requirement 6

We apply security measures to protect information processed when staff work remotely.

### Requirement 7

We apply security measures to protect information stored when staff work remotely.

### Requirement 8

We require a corporate VPN with MFA authentication for all remote access, corporate network and corporate resources.

### Requirement 9

We manage remote work devices through MDM/UEM solutions with enforced security policies.

### Requirement 10

We require disk encryption, inactivity locking and restriction of unauthorized applications on all remote devices.

### Requirement 11

We perform periodic security policy compliance assessments on remote devices.
