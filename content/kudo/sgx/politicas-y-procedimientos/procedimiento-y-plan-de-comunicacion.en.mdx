description: "Communication strategy for incidents and crises"
---

## Purpose
Establish an internal and external communication process that ensures all employees and relevant interested parties are informed and engaged.

## Scope
Applies to all processes within the organization.

## Procedure

### General
The organization maintains a communication plan that considers:
- Audience diversity (gender, language, culture, literacy, disability).
- Legal and regulatory requirements.
- The perspectives of external interested parties.

### The plan defines:
- Communication content.
- Target audience (employees, vendors, partners, regulators, etc.).
- Frequency.
- Communication channels.

### Internal Communication
- Aimed at employees and staff at all levels.
- Includes changes to plans, policies, audits, roles and responsibilities, emergency plans, incidents, corrective actions and improvement projects.
- Channels: email, meetings, training, induction and displays.

### External Communication
- Aimed at contractors, vendors, subcontractors, statutory bodies and other interested parties.
- Includes the organization's services, emergency plans, management review results, incidents and corrective actions, and regulatory or contractual requirements.
- Channels: media, email and meetings.

### Response and Records
- All inquiries must be answered in a timely manner.
- Records of relevant communication (digital or scanned) must be kept for traceability.

## Monitoring and Review
- Periodically review and update the communication plan to ensure its effectiveness.
- Oversight by Management and responsible teams.
- Evaluate effectiveness and adjust the plan based on feedback, legal changes or the organization's needs.
