## What is this course for?

Digital investigation methodologies applied to security incidents.

## What will I learn?

- 🚀 **Technology Investigation Fundamentals**: Learn the scope, legal framework and profile of the technology investigator.
- 🚀 **Investigation Methodology**: Apply a structured methodology, from the hypothesis to the use of OSINT.
- 🚀 **Digital Tracing**: Trace IP addresses, metadata and activity on social networks and mobile devices.
- 🚀 **Collecting and Preserving Digital Evidence**: Identify and preserve digital evidence while respecting the chain of custody.
- 🚀 **Evidence Analysis**: Correlate evidence and reconstruct the timeline of events.
- 🚀 **Reporting and Closing the Investigation**: Write clear reports and present findings to non-technical audiences.
