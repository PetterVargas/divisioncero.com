## What is this course for?

Structure, processes and operation of incident response teams.

## What will I learn?

- 🚀 **CSIRT/SIRT Fundamentals**: Learn the differences between a CSIRT, SIRT and CERT, and their typical services.
- 🚀 **Team Design and Formation**: Define the team's mission, structure and executive sponsorship.
- 🚀 **Processes and Operational Capabilities**: Design the CSIRT's processes, tools and service level agreements.
- 🚀 **Collaboration and Trust Networks**: Take part in CSIRT communities and share threat information.
- 🚀 **Team Maturity and Assessment**: Assess the team's maturity with models such as SIM3 and plan its improvement.
