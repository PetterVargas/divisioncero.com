## Purpose
Establish standards for the secure and efficient use of endpoint devices and IT resources, protecting data and supporting the organization's mission.

## Scope
Applies to all users, contractors and devices that access IT resources, including laptops, mobile phones, cloud systems and platforms that process organizational data.

## Standards

### Responsible Use of Resources
- Handle IT assets securely and responsibly.
- Register all assets in the Asset Inventory and maintain network and configuration documentation.
- Immediately report losses, thefts or suspicious activity.
- Implement procedures for secure data deletion and device reuse.

### Device and Network Security
- Keep systems up to date with anti-malware software and vulnerability management.
- Configure firewalls, encryption and secure authentication on all devices.
- Isolate critical subnets in the event of attacks and disable vulnerable protocols.
- Segregate network administration from normal traffic and continuously monitor network security.

### Clear Desk and Clear Screen Requirements
- Keep sensitive information under lock and key and protect devices when not in use.
- Configure automatic logout and collect printouts immediately.
- Erase whiteboards and screens after use.
- Perform final sweeps when vacating offices or remote workspaces.

### Laptops
- Keep software up to date and apply secure password practices.
- Lock devices after inactivity and use approved encryption.
- Protect confidential data and securely delete information when returning devices.
- Training in the secure use of online resources and web filtering.

### Mobile Phones
- Use secure authentication and encryption; securely delete data before disposing of the device.
- Install security and web filtering tools.
- Training in secure web use and compliance with filtering policies.

### Remote Work
- Ensure a secure physical environment and protected storage of sensitive information.
- Use VPN, encrypted communication and secure remote access solutions.
- Follow backup, firewall and anti-malware protocols.
- Compliance monitoring and auditing, and revocation of access when employment ends.

### Equipment Maintenance
- Maintain equipment according to the vendor's specifications, with authorized and supervised staff.
- Document failures and preventive and corrective maintenance.
- Implement confidentiality agreements and security measures for remote or off-site maintenance.
- Apply secure equipment disposal procedures.

## Monitoring and Review
- Periodic inspections of IT assets to ensure compliance.
- Immediate correction of deviations.
- Annual review of the policy to keep it aligned with threats and cybersecurity best practices.
