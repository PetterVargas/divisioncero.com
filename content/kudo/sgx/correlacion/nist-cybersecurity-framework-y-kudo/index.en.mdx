The NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) 2.0 is a voluntary framework developed by the National Institute of Standards and Technology to manage and reduce cybersecurity risk in organizations of any size and sector. Version 2.0 (2024) introduces the GOVERN function as the central axis of governance. Kudo maps its functions and subcategories to the framework and the ISMS policies.
