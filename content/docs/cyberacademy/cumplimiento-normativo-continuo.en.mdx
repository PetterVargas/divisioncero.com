## What is this course for?

ISO 27001, NIST CSF, GDPR and local regulations, on a continuous basis.

## What will I learn?

- 🚀 **Regulatory Compliance Fundamentals**: Learn the regulatory framework and the difference between point-in-time and continuous compliance.
- 🚀 **Requirements Mapping and Management**: Map regulatory requirements to internal controls and regulatory frameworks.
- 🚀 **Continuous Compliance Monitoring**: Automate control monitoring and manage non-compliance findings.
- 🚀 **Audit and Assessment Management**: Prepare for audits and manage remediation plans.
- 🚀 **Compliance Program Culture and Improvement**: Communicate compliance and report it to senior management.

## Projects

With this course you'll create, optimize, automate or improve the following projects:

- 🚀 You'll create an internal site (employees) for cybersecurity compliance management.
- 🚀 You'll create an external site (customers) for cybersecurity compliance management.
- 🚀 Collaborative mapping of regulatory compliance and cybersecurity best practices.
