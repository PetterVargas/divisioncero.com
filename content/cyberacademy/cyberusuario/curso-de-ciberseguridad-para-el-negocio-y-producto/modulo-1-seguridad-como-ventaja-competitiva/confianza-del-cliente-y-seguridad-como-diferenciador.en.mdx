---
title: "Customer Trust and Security as a Differentiator"
description: "Why security builds trust and sales"
---

When two products offer similar features, security becomes a factor that can tip the customer's decision, especially in sectors such as finance, health or any business that handles sensitive information, where a competitor's previous data leak can be exactly the argument the sales team needs to close a deal. Communicating security as a differentiator doesn't mean filling the website with technical jargon, but translating concrete practices, such as data encryption or certifications obtained, into clear benefits the customer understands, such as "your information is never shared without your authorization". Companies that treat security as part of their value proposition, instead of a hidden cost, tend to have shorter sales cycles with corporate customers that require evidence of good practices before signing any contract.
