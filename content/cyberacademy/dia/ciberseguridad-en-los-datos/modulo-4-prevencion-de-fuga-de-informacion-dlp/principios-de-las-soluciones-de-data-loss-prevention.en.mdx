---
title: "Principles of Data Loss Prevention Solutions"
description: "Basic principles of DLP solutions"
---

Data Loss Prevention solutions aim to detect and block the unauthorized exit of sensitive information outside the organization's perimeter, whether by email, cloud storage, USB devices or even screenshots. They work based on rules and patterns, such as regular expressions to identify card numbers or ID documents, fingerprints of specific documents, and content classification based on the context of use. A well-implemented DLP doesn't just block; it also alerts and helps understand the real flow of sensitive information within the organization, revealing business processes that depend on sharing data in ways nobody had documented. The main challenge is calibrating the rules: a DLP that's too strict generates constant blocks that frustrate users and push them to find ways around it, while one that's too lax lets through exactly what it was supposed to stop.
