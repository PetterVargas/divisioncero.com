Create a tool for explaining and identifying the CIA triad: Confidentiality, Integrity and Availability

Team, these are the three most mentioned words in the context of information security: 

Confidentiality: only authorized people can access the information.
Integrity: data isn't altered or manipulated without permission.
Availability: systems and data are accessible when needed.

Team, rather than memorizing a definition, what interests me in this space is that we identify when one or more of the components of the CIA triad is affected; in this tool we can do practical exercises to identify when some of them are affected.
