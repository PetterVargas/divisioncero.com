---
title: "Logging and Monitoring Policy"
description: "Event logging and security monitoring of systems"
---

## Purpose
Ensure effective oversight of systems and applications, preserving the integrity, confidentiality and availability of data, and improving the detection of anomalous events and threats.

## Scope
Applies to all systems, applications and cloud infrastructure, including Ubuntu EC2 instance logs, applications piped to CloudWatch, network traffic monitors and external threat sources.

## Principles
- Complete and secure logging of critical events.
- Aggregation and correlation of data from multiple sources for anomaly detection.
- Secure storage and retention of logs according to defined periods.
- Periodic testing of data discovery effectiveness, especially for sensitive information (e.g. PAN).

## System Configuration
- All systems and applications must log essential information.
- Data must be confidential, intact and accessible only to authorized staff.
- Logs are preserved according to the retention policy and securely deleted after evaluation.

## Monitoring
- Automatic scripts analyze events every five minutes in CloudWatch.
- Correlation engines detect complex threat patterns.
- Immediate notifications to the relevant staff through designated channels (e.g. Slack).
- Incident investigation according to the Incident Management Policy.

## Review and Analysis
- Periodic log analysis to confirm effectiveness and detect threats.
- Systematic recording of identified inconsistencies and improvements.
- Annual documentation of data discovery effectiveness tests.

## Response and Investigation
- All incidents are thoroughly analyzed and documented.
- Findings drive continuous improvements to logging and monitoring systems.

## Policy Monitoring and Review
- Annual review or upon significant changes to systems or processes.
- Frequent audits and assessments to stay relevant in the face of threats and technological change.
