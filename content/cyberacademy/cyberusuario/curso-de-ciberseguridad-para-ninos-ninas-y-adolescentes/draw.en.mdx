we share is for you, because we start using a phone, a computer or any technological device without knowing that there are bad people 
on the other side of that profile we think is "*our friend*", who are only looking for a moment of weakness on our part, that moment when 
we lack trust in our parents, responsible adults or loved ones, so that cybercriminal can achieve their goals.
