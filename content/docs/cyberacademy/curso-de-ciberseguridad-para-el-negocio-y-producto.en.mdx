## What is this course for?

Hi team, in this space we're going to apply cybersecurity to business strategy and the product lifecycle.

## What will I learn?

- 🚀 **Security as a Competitive Advantage**: Discover how security strengthens customer trust and the business.
- 🚀 **Security in Product Design**: Build security and privacy into the earliest stages of the product.
- 🚀 **Data and Business Models**: Responsibly manage customer data and its legal compliance.
- 🚀 **Security in Partnerships and Third Parties**: Assess the security of vendors, partners and external integrations.
- 🚀 **Communicating Risk to the Business**: Learn to translate technical risks into business decisions.

## Projects

With this course you'll create, optimize, automate or improve the following projects:

- 🚀 You'll identify the security requirements enterprise customers demand (SSO, SAML, RBAC).
- 🚀 You'll answer security questionnaires and RFPs with confidence.
- 🚀 You'll communicate compliance certifications (SOC 2, ISO 27001, GDPR) to prospects and customers.
- 🚀 You'll design a product roadmap with prioritized security features.
- 🚀 You'll define pricing and packaging strategies for enterprise cybersecurity features.
- 🚀 You'll develop processes for communicating incidents to customers.
- 🚀 You'll establish cybersecurity SLAs and secure onboarding processes for enterprise customers.
