---
title: "Differences between AI Security and Traditional Security"
description: "What sets AI security apart from classic security"
---

Traditional security focuses on protecting code, networks and systems against known, exploitable vulnerabilities through patches and perimeter controls. AI security adds a different attack surface: training data, model weights and inference behavior become targets, and threats don't always exploit a code flaw but the system's own statistical logic, as happens with adversarial examples or prompts designed to manipulate a response. In addition, many attacks on AI models are hard to detect with classic security tools because they leave no recognizable signatures, and their impact can be subtle, such as a gradually introduced bias. That's why security teams need to build machine learning knowledge and collaborate closely with data teams, something uncommon in conventional infrastructure security.
