## Purpose
This policy sets out the guidelines to ensure the continuity of the organization's critical functions during and after a disruption. It ensures staff know the principles of business continuity, risk management, incident response and compliance. Compliance is mandatory for all staff and applies to all areas.

## Objectives
- Identify critical processes and services and ensure their continuity in the event of a disruption.
- Minimize the impact on the organization, customers and interested parties.
- Ensure the fast, orderly recovery of essential operations.
- Align the BCMS with the strategy and regulatory requirements.
- Establish a clear crisis management structure.
- Maintain an up-to-date, practical BCMS under continuous improvement.

## Scope
This policy covers:
- All locations, offices and business units.
- Essential functions such as IT, Finance, Operations, HR and other key areas.
- Critical processes and necessary resources (equipment, facilities, applications, knowledge).
- Relevant risks: natural disasters, cyberattacks, technical failures, human error.
- Training and awareness programs for all staff.

## Standard

### Risk Assessment
Identify threats and vulnerabilities that may affect continuity. Determine critical processes and minimum operational requirements.

### Business Continuity Plan (BCP)
Develop plans for each critical function, including recovery steps, roles, communication and safeguarding of information. Plans must be tested and updated periodically.

### Incident Response
Activate response procedures in the event of disruptions to restore services with the least possible impact and recover critical systems, data and processes.

### Training and Awareness
- Mandatory annual training.
- Regular awareness campaigns.
- Induction training for new employees.
- Refresher sessions according to relevant changes.
- Up-to-date records of all training.

### Legal and Regulatory Compliance
The BCMS must comply with applicable requirements such as GDPR, HIPAA, BRRD or other regulators depending on the jurisdiction.

### Business Impact Analysis (BIA)
Carry out a full BIA every three years, with annual reviews to adjust priorities and recovery times.

### Vendor Assessment
Identify critical vendors and validate their compliance with continuity agreements, especially during the BIA and periodic reviews.

### Exercises and Testing
Carry out regular BC/DR exercises to validate recovery times, roles and the effectiveness of the plans.

### Incident Response Structure
Define how serious incidents are managed, such as:
- Loss of a data center
- Loss of offices or facilities
- Failures of critical vendors
- Absence of essential staff

### Competencies
Define the skills required for BCMS roles and ensure they're met through ongoing training.

### Performance Evaluation
Measure BCMS performance through KPIs and report them to the Continuity Committees to prioritize improvements.

### Audit and Assessment
Carry out internal and external audits to validate the effectiveness of the BCMS. Audits must cover all areas at least once every 3 years.

### Continuous Improvement
Identify corrective actions, non-conformities and improvement opportunities, apply them and verify their effectiveness in each review cycle.

## Protection Against Utility Failures
To ensure continuity, facilities and systems must be protected against power or utility failures through:
- Equipment configured and maintained according to specification.
- Periodic capacity assessments in light of growth.
- Regular inspections and tests.
- Failure alarm systems.
- Redundant power and diverse routes.
- Network separation between critical equipment and IT systems.
- Protection of internet-connected equipment.
- Emergency lighting and communications available.

## Resource Commitment
Management defines the resources needed for continuity annually and Finance guarantees the required funds.

## Communication
The BCMS Manager, with Internal Communications, manages communication about the program. In emergencies, the established crisis communication strategy is followed to protect the corporate reputation.

## Compliance
Compliance with this policy is mandatory. If there are stricter laws or standards, they prevail.

## Monitoring and Review
The organization maintains a process to evaluate the effectiveness of the BCMS through:
- Regular review of documents, policies and controls.
- Evaluation of legal and contractual compliance.
- Periodic reports to management on BCMS performance.
- Ongoing evaluation of internal controls and timely communication of deficiencies.
- Verification and follow-up of corrective actions.
- Periodic internal audits and effectiveness reviews.

Management reviews the results and ensures improvements and adjustments are implemented when needed to maintain the organization's continuity and resilience.
