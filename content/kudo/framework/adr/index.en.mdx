## Domain Description

Domain that establishes the **capabilities for continuous monitoring, proactive threat detection and structured 
response to security incidents**. It includes controls for *vulnerability management, identification 
and remediation, retention and monitoring of activity logs, and the formal handling of cybersecurity events and 
incidents through playbooks and response plans*. It integrates *SIEM, threat intelligence, threat 
hunting and digital forensics* capabilities.
