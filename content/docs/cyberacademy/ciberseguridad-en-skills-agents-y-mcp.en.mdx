## What is this course for?

Security for AI agents, skills and the MCP protocol.

## What will I learn?

- 🚀 **AI Agents and Skills Fundamentals**: Learn about AI agents, skills and the MCP protocol.
- 🚀 **AI Agent Attack Surface**: Identify prompt injection risks and the risks of permissions granted to agents.
- 🚀 **Security of the MCP Protocol and Its Integrations**: Secure the connection and credentials between agents and MCP servers.
- 🚀 **Controls and Best Practices**: Apply least privilege, sandboxing and traceability to the agent's actions.
- 🚀 **Governance of Agent-Based Solutions**: Assess risk and define human oversight mechanisms for your agents.
