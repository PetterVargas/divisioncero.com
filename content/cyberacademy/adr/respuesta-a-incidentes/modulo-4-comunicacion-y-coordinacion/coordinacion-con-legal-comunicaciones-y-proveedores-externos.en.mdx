---
title: "Coordinating with Legal, Communications and External Providers"
description: "Aligning all the areas involved"
---

A significant security incident is rarely resolved by the technical team alone, and effectively coordinating with legal, communications and external providers such as specialized forensic firms or cyber risk insurers usually determines both the speed of recovery and the organization's final legal exposure. In practice, legal should get involved early to preserve legal privilege over certain investigation documents and to guide notification decisions, while communications needs to prepare consistent messages before information leaks uncontrollably to the media or social networks. Bringing in external providers, such as a specialized forensic team, requires predefined activation processes, because negotiating contracts and access in the middle of the crisis delays the response just when speed is most needed. A key nuance is that these external teams must work under the coordination of the internal incident lead, not in a parallel, disconnected way, to prevent the investigation from fragmenting into different versions of what happened.
