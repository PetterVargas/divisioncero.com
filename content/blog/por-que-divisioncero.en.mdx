## Why DivisionCero

We aim to provide continuous training and apply it to business situations.


## Our Principles

- 🚀 Be honest, even if it means losing customers.
- 🚀 If what we build doesn't solve a problem, it won't be on our roadmap.
- 🚀 Build tools that work, are realistic and applicable to today's context.
- 🚀 Make cybersecurity more accessible.
- 🚀 And yes, the Zen of Python is our point of reference.


## What we're after

- ✅ Courses optimized for problem solving.
- ✅ Protecting the main attack vector: humans.
- ✅ Improving the skills of CyberGuardians.
- ✅ Building tools that make our lives easier.
- ✅ Cybersecurity methodology and automation.


## What we're not after

- 👎🏽 Creating lots of courses to expand our catalog. _Clarification: We prefer fewer, but effective courses that work in the real world._
- 👎🏽 Focusing on multiple fields. _Clarification: Only those related to Cybersecurity._
- 👎🏽 Courses that haven't been updated for long periods of time. _Clarification: The course you take today could be completely different in two months due to continuous updates._
