## What is DivisionCero Self-hosting?

Self-hosting is the option where **your organization installs and runs DivisionCero on its own infrastructure**, instead of using it as a service (CSaaS). You control the server, the data and the update cycle; DivisionCero provides the software and the license to use it.

### When should you choose Self-hosting?

- 🔒 **Data control**: your organization's information stays on a server you manage.
- 🏢 **Internal or regulatory requirements**: your policies require cybersecurity tools to be under your management.
- 🛠️ **Technical team available**: you have people who can manage a server, the domain and backups.

If you'd rather not manage infrastructure, the [CSaaS](/en/precios?modelo=csaas) model is the recommended option.
