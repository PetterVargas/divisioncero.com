## What is this course for?

Collection, preservation and analysis of digital evidence.

## What will I learn?

- 🚀 **Digital Forensics Fundamentals**: Learn forensic principles and the types of analysis: disk, memory, network and mobile.
- 🚀 **Evidence Acquisition**: Acquire digital evidence while preserving its integrity and chain of custody.
- 🚀 **System Forensic Analysis**: Analyze file systems, RAM and deleted or hidden data.
- 🚀 **Network and Mobile Device Forensics**: Analyze network traffic and mobile devices, and correlate evidence.
- 🚀 **Expert Reporting and Presentation**: Write expert reports and support findings before legal audiences.
