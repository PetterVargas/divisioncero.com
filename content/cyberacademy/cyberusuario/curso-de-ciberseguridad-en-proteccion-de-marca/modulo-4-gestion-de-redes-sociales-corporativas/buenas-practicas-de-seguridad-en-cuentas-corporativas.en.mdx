---
title: "Security Best Practices for Corporate Accounts"
description: "Key practices for protecting corporate accounts"
---

Protecting corporate social media accounts requires simple but consistent habits, such as enabling two-step verification, using unique, strong passwords for each platform and periodically reviewing which external apps have connected access to the account. Often the weak point isn't the platform itself, but forgotten third-party integrations, such as a post scheduling tool that's no longer used but still has active permissions and can be an attacker's way in. Adopting these practices systematically, with regular reviews and clear owners for each account, greatly reduces the likelihood of the brand suffering an account takeover that ends up publishing offensive or fraudulent content in the company's name.
