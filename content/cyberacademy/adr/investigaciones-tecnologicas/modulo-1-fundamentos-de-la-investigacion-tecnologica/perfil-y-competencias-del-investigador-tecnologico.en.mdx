---
title: "Profile and Competencies of the Technology Investigator"
description: "Key skills of a good investigator"
---

A competent technology investigator combines deep technical knowledge with skills that tend to be underestimated, such as clear writing, critical thinking and handling pressure under legal or executive scrutiny. In technical terms, mastery of operating systems, networks, forensic analysis and evidence collection tools is expected, but in practice what sets an effective investigator apart is their ability to stay objective, document each step reproducibly and communicate technical findings to audiences who don't know the field's jargon. Professional ethics is another non-negotiable pillar, since the investigator handles sensitive information and their conclusions can affect careers, legal proceedings or business decisions. A typical case where this is put to the test is when the evidence points to a colleague or superior: the investigator must follow the same methodological rigor without letting personal relationships or organizational pressure alter their conclusions.
