The Cybersecurity for Business and Product Course is part of the CyberUsuario learning path; we wrote this description to give you a 
mind map of what we're achieving and how everything is part of an ecosystem focused on continuous improvement. 

I want you to imagine you're on a roller coaster, and as you go through the ups and downs the cart starts falling apart or becomes too small for the next 
hill; that's what the corporate context is like: while we have a customer base to sustain and grow in a constantly changing market, this course 
aims for us to fix and improve the cart while we're on that roller coaster, without going off the rails.
