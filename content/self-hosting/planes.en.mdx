## Self-hosting plans

The plan depends on your organization's **number of employees** and its **annual revenue**. All plans include installation on your own infrastructure.

| Plan | Employees | Monthly | Annual | Includes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Community** | Up to 9 | Free | Free | Community edition |
| **Company of 10 to 50** | 10 to 50 | $112.99 USD/month | $499 USD/year | Enterprise edition |
| **Company of 50+** | More than 50 | $224.99 USD/month | $999 USD/year | Enterprise edition and Single Sign-On (SSO) |

<Callout title="Save with annual billing">
  Annual billing is roughly a 63% saving compared with monthly billing.
</Callout>

### Community

- Free and available on [GitHub](https://github.com/PetterVargas/divisioncero).
- For organizations with **up to 9 employees**.
- **Not available** for companies with annual revenue above **100K USD**, even if they have fewer than 9 employees.

### Company of 10 to 50

- For organizations with **10 to 50 employees**.
- Required for companies with annual revenue above 100K USD.

### Company of 50+

- For organizations with **more than 50 employees**.
- Includes **Single Sign-On (SSO)** to centralize access with your organization's identity provider.

### How do I get a plan?

Choose the plan and billing type on the [pricing page](/en/precios?modelo=self-hosting) and contact us on WhatsApp; we'll help you with the purchase and license activation.
