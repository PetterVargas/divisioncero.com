## What is this course for?

Reference frameworks and the roles and responsibilities of the CISO.

## What will I learn?

- 🚀 **Cybersecurity Governance Fundamentals**: Distinguish between security governance, management and operations.
- 🚀 **Designing the Cybersecurity Strategy**: Align the security strategy with business objectives.
- 🚀 **Policies and Internal Regulatory Framework**: Design the security policy architecture and manage its lifecycle.
- 🚀 **Budget and Resource Management**: Prepare the budget and justify the return on security investment.
- 🚀 **Governance Measurement and Reporting**: Measure security governance and report it to senior management and the board.
