---
title: "Participating in CSIRT Communities and Networks"
description: "Networks such as FIRST and regional CSIRTs"
---

Being part of networks such as FIRST (Forum of Incident Response and Security Teams) or regional CSIRT forums gives access to early warnings, best practices and trusted contacts that are invaluable during an incident that crosses organizational boundaries. These communities run on trust relationships built over time, not just formal memberships: taking part in meetings, joint exercises and thematic working groups is what really builds that trust. Membership in a recognized network also brings the team external credibility, makes coordination easier in incidents affecting multiple organizations in the same sector, and offers a channel for requesting specialized help in scenarios that exceed internal capacity, such as coordinated attack campaigns or advanced persistent threats.
