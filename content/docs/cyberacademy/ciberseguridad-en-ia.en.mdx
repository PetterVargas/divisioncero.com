## What is this course for?

Risks, governance and protection of artificial intelligence systems.

## What will I learn?

- 🚀 **Artificial Intelligence Security Fundamentals**: Learn the security risk landscape of AI systems.
- 🚀 **Security in the Model Lifecycle**: Protect training data and deploy models securely.
- 🚀 **Threats Specific to AI Models**: Identify prompt injection, model theft and evasion attacks.
- 🚀 **Privacy and Ethics in AI**: Manage privacy, algorithmic bias and responsible AI.
- 🚀 **AI Security Governance**: Define policies and continuously monitor AI systems in production.

## Projects

With this course you'll create, optimize, automate or improve the following projects:

- 🚀 You'll develop AI governance frameworks with risk assessments and algorithmic audits.
