---
title: "Objectives and Scope of a Cybersecurity Audit"
description: "Defining the why and how far"
---

Before running any test, a cybersecurity audit needs clear objectives, for example verifying compliance with a standard, evaluating the effectiveness of technical controls or giving leadership assurance about a specific risk, and a scope that defines which systems, processes or business units are actually covered. Without this prior definition, the audit team commonly ends up reviewing what's most accessible instead of what's most relevant, and the results don't answer the question that originally prompted the audit. In practice, the scope should be negotiated with stakeholders and documented in writing, explicitly including what's left out, since poorly communicated exclusions are a frequent source of conflict when an incident occurs in an area the business assumed was covered but the auditor never assessed.
