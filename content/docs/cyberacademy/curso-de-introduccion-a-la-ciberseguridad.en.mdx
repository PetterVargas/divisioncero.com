## What is this course for?

Hi team, good day, and welcome to this course we've built for anyone as a first step into the wonderful world of cybersecurity. You don't need any prior knowledge, just to be in **#EagerToLearn** mode and aware that cybersecurity is everyone's job: however trivial we consider the situation, we can protect many people.

## What will I learn?

- 🚀 **Cybersecurity Fundamentals**: Essential concepts for understanding and applying cybersecurity.
- 🚀 **Threats and Malicious Actors**: Identify the main threats and who's behind them.
- 🚀 **Passwords and Digital Identity**: Protect your accounts with strong passwords and multi-factor authentication.
- 🚀 **Secure Browsing and Communication**: Browse safely and avoid online fraud.
- 🚀 **Cybersecurity Culture and Incident Reporting**: Promote secure habits and learn to report incidents.

## Projects

With this course you'll create, optimize, automate or improve the following projects:

- 🚀 You'll set up two-factor authentication (2FA) on your main accounts.
- 🚀 You'll implement a secure password manager for all your credentials.
- 🚀 You'll make secure backups of your critical information.
- 🚀 You'll learn best practices for online shopping and payments.
