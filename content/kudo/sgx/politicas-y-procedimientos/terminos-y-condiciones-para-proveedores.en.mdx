## Purpose
Establish the operational conditions every vendor must accept and comply with, organized according to the **eight domains of the Kudo framework**, so that each area of the organization can require from the vendor what corresponds to its risk domain.

## Scope
Applies to every vendor onboarded in accordance with the [Vendor Management Procedure](/en/kudo/sgx/politicas-y-procedimientos/procedimiento-de-gestion-de-proveedores). A vendor is only subject to the domains relevant to the service it provides.

## COR - Organizational Coherence
- The vendor accepts the ISMS policies that apply to it and the statements made in its Onboarding Form.
- It must report corporate changes, changes in shareholder control or legal representation that affect the contractual relationship.
- It cooperates with the risk assessments and compliance audits the organization carries out on its service.

## CAP - Application Cybersecurity
- If it develops or maintains software, APIs or integrations for the organization, it must follow secure development lifecycle (SDLC) practices.
- It must declare the third-party and open-source components used, and remediate critical or high vulnerabilities identified before deployment.
- It must report vulnerabilities discovered in its deliverables during the term of the contract.

## CIP - Identity and Endpoint Cybersecurity
- The vendor's staff must use individual, non-shared credentials to access the organization's systems.
- Remote access to the organization's systems requires multi-factor authentication (MFA).
- Granted access is revoked immediately when the service ends or when the vendor's employee who used it leaves.

## CIF - Infrastructure Cybersecurity
- If it provides cloud or infrastructure services, the vendor must comply with the agreed shared responsibility model and communicate it explicitly.
- Information in transit and at rest under its custody must be encrypted with current standards.
- It must maintain segmentation between the organization's environments and those of other customers.

## THP - Human Talent and Vendors
- Vendor staff providing on-site or remote services must receive basic information security training before starting activities.
- The vendor verifies the background of its own staff according to the risk of the service provided.
- Vendor staff sign their own confidentiality agreements, which don't release the vendor from its contractual liability.

## CCN - Business Continuity and Change
- The vendor gives reasonable advance notice of changes that may affect the continuity or quality of the service.
- It has its own continuity plan proportional to the criticality of the service provided.
- Maintenance windows affecting the organization are agreed and communicated in advance.

## ADR - Cybersecurity Analysis, Detection and Response
- The vendor notifies the organization of any security incident affecting its data, systems or services within 48 hours of detection.
- It cooperates with the organization in investigating incidents involving its service.
- It proactively reports known vulnerabilities that may affect the organization.

## DIA - Data and Artificial Intelligence Cybersecurity
- The vendor processes personal data in accordance with applicable regulations and solely for the purposes authorized by the organization.
- It doesn't use the organization's data to train or retrain artificial intelligence models without prior written authorization.
- It reports the geographic location where it processes or stores the organization's data.
- It deletes or returns the organization's data at the end of the contract, certifying the deletion when requested.

## Acceptance and Validity
Accepting these Terms and Conditions is a prerequisite for onboarding the vendor and is formalized in the Vendor Onboarding Form. They remain valid for the term of the signed contract.

## Monitoring and Review
These terms are reviewed annually together with the [Vendor Management Policy](/en/kudo/sgx/politicas-y-procedimientos/politica-de-gestion-de-proveedores) and upon changes to the Kudo framework domains.
