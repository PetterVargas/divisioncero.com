The Cybersecurity for Parents and Responsible Adults Course is part of the CyberUsuario learning path; we wrote this description to give you a 
mind map of what we're achieving and how everything is part of an ecosystem focused on continuous improvement. 

Parents, responsible adults, professionals, teachers, relatives, loved ones, I'll stop listing here, because I wouldn't finish the list today; but 
this message is for you: we all have a moral obligation to protect children, they're our primary asset as a society. Just as in Japan young 
children can walk the streets without their parents, let's strive for that everywhere; each of us, as part of a society, 
must guarantee the protection of our children, and cyberspace is no exception.
