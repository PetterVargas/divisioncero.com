## Domain Description

Domain dedicated to the protection and secure management of the **complete software lifecycle** (SDLC), including 
**scenarios with AI agents**. Through *business-based threat modeling* it integrates security controls from 
the *ideation, design and development* phases through to the deployment and operation of applications and APIs. It covers 
DevSecOps practices, static (SAST) and dynamic (DAST) vulnerability analysis, automation in CI/CD pipelines, 
branch protection and codeowners, and securing application programming interfaces (APIs). 
It aims for **every team in the company to build cybersecurity skills** to *identify and mitigate risks 
before they reach production, reducing security technical debt and protecting the software supply chain.*

## CAP Controls

Protection and secure management of the complete application and software development lifecycle.
