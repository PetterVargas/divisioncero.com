---
title: COR-1 - Risk Management and Cybersecurity Program
description: Strategic governance control for cybersecurity and organizational risk
---

## Control Definition

An Information Security Management System must be created, defining its scope based on the organizational context and the needs 
of its stakeholders, and a cybersecurity lead must be appointed with roles, resources and a governance structure periodically reviewed by 
senior management. The management system's policies, processes and procedures must be documented, approved and communicated, and a 
risk management methodology must be implemented to identify and prioritize risks and assign each an owner and a treatment plan. Cybersecurity 
initiatives must be aligned with business objectives through a prioritized roadmap, their progress and management indicators must be reported 
periodically to senior management, and continuous improvement of the system must be driven by leadership.

## Requirements

### Requirement 1

We create the Information Security Management System (ISMS).

### Requirement 2

We define the scope of the ISMS considering the context, the stakeholders and its connection with other organizations.

### Requirement 3

We identify the internal, external and third-party factors that can affect the ISMS and the achievement of its goals.

### Requirement 4

We identify the ISMS stakeholders.

### Requirement 5

We identify the needs and expectations of the ISMS stakeholders.

### Requirement 6

We determine which stakeholder needs and expectations will be addressed by the ISMS.

### Requirement 7

We appoint a cybersecurity lead in the company.

### Requirement 8

We define the roles and responsibilities or the RACI matrix of the ISMS.

### Requirement 9

We ensure the ISMS has the resources it needs to operate.

### Requirement 10

We create an information security policy that fits what the organization does and needs.

### Requirement 11

We create an information security policy that includes clear security objectives or serves as a guide for defining them.

### Requirement 12

We create an information security policy that includes a commitment to comply with all the rules, requirements and stakeholders defined by the organization.

### Requirement 13

We create an information security policy that includes a commitment to continually improve the management system.

### Requirement 14

We create and continually improve the documents on the company's policies.

### Requirement 15

We create and continually improve the documents on the company's processes.

### Requirement 16

We create and continually improve the documents on the company's procedures.

### Requirement 17

We document the ISMS domains, controls and requirements.

### Requirement 18

We approve, communicate and version the ISMS documents.

### Requirement 19

We ensure stakeholders acknowledge and know the ISMS documents.

### Requirement 20

We integrate the ISMS requirements into the organization's processes.

### Requirement 21

We establish a procedure for the centralized management and collection of evidence for cybersecurity requirements and controls, ensuring its availability to stakeholders.

### Requirement 22

We implement a risk management methodology.

### Requirement 23

We declare the organization's risk appetite.

### Requirement 24

We declare the organization's risk tolerance.

### Requirement 25

We incorporate asset, threat and vulnerability valuation criteria into the risk methodology.

### Requirement 26

We assign each risk an owner, a residual risk level and a treatment plan.

### Requirement 27

We draw up a cybersecurity roadmap.

### Requirement 28

We prioritize cybersecurity initiatives with owners and implementation dates.

### Requirement 29

We align cybersecurity initiatives with business objectives.

### Requirement 30

We record identified risks in a centralized repository.

### Requirement 31

We review the governance structure annually and submit it for senior management approval.

### Requirement 32

We hold security committees or report to senior management on a scheduled basis.

### Requirement 33

We include in management reports and reviews the status of critical risks, roadmap progress, incident indicators, regulatory compliance, control performance, non-compliance trends, measurement and audit results, achievement of objectives, risk assessment results, progress on the risk treatment plan and progress on improvements.

### Requirement 34

We define and track information security and cybersecurity OKRs.

### Requirement 35

We define and track information security and cybersecurity KPIs.

### Requirement 36

We continually improve the ISMS, and review and update its documents on a scheduled basis together with their stakeholders.

### Requirement 37

Leadership drives the continuous improvement of the ISMS.
