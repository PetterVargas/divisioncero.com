## Domain Description

Domain focused on **access control, digital identity management and endpoint device protection**. 
It covers the complete *identity inventory, multi-factor authentication (MFA), role-based authorization 
(RBAC, ABAC, PBAC), user provisioning and deprovisioning, as well as the inventory and hardening 
of endpoints*. It promotes the **Zero Trust** strategy as its guiding principle, prioritizing the management 
of **100% cloud identities** and integration with **Single Sign-On (SSO)**. 
It's considered the **organization's most relevant initial protection pillar**, since identity represents 
*the main attack vector in modern environments.*
