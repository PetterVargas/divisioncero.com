## Domain Description

Domain that manages the **human factor and the supply chain as critical cybersecurity risk vectors**. 
It covers *ongoing cybersecurity training for employees, the controls associated with 
remote work, and the supply chain inventory and assessment*, managing the shared responsibility model with 
vendors and third parties. It includes *awareness campaigns, phishing simulations and the automation of user provisioning 
and deprovisioning.*
It recognizes **humans as the most frequently exploited attack vector**, and **vendors as an extension 
of the organization's risk perimeter**.

## THP Controls

Secure management of people, cybersecurity training and risk control in the supply chain.
