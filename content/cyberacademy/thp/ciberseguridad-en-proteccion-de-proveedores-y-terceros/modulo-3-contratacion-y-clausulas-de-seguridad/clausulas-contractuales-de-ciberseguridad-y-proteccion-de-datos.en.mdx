---
title: "Cybersecurity and Data Protection Contract Clauses"
description: "Security clauses a contract should have"
---

A contract with a vendor that processes data or accesses the organization's systems must include specific cybersecurity clauses, since a generic mention of confidentiality isn't enough, including the obligation to implement minimum security controls, report incidents within a defined time frame, allow audits, and establish clear responsibilities in the event of a breach involving the organization's data. These clauses must also cover personal data protection in accordance with applicable regulations, specifying roles such as data processor or controller, and the conditions under which the vendor may subcontract other third parties with access to that same data. Drafting these clauses after signing the contract is practically impossible, so involving the security team in the negotiation stage, and not just the legal area, keeps the organization from being left without contractual tools when a vendor suffers an incident that directly affects it.
