---
title: "ICS/OT Security Policy"
description: "Security of industrial control systems and operational technology"
---

## Purpose
Protect Industrial Control Systems (ICS) and Operational Technology (OT) against cyber threats, ensuring continuous operation and the integrity, confidentiality and availability of the systems.

## Scope
Applies to all ICS/OT assets (controllers, sensors, actuators, network devices, software and associated equipment) and to all staff, contractors and authorized users who interact with these systems.

## Objectives
- Strengthen ICS/OT security.
- Align ICS/OT operations with the overall cybersecurity strategy.
- Ensure resilience and rapid incident response.
- Protect the integrity, confidentiality and availability of ICS/OT assets.

## Key Standards

**Asset and Connection Management**
- Document all ICS/OT devices in the asset inventory.
- Maintain strict physical and logical segmentation between ICS networks and other corporate or external networks.
- Restrict the use of external media and mobile devices; subject them to security scans if they're required.

**Networks and Systems**
- Continuously monitor the ICS/OT network and keep security event logs.
- Configure and harden systems against known vulnerabilities.
- Protect Safety Instrumented Systems (SIS) through isolation and strict controls.
- Manage vulnerabilities and patches in a way adapted to ICS/OT systems.
- Use dedicated cybersecurity applications for malware detection and mitigation.

**Data and Applications**
- Classify and handle ICS/OT data according to sensitivity and operational importance.
- Use software and applications from trusted vendors, periodically assessing vulnerabilities.

**Employees and Contractors**
- Mandatory training in ICS/OT cybersecurity practices.
- Immediate reporting of anomalies, failures or cyber threats.

## Monitoring and Review
- Regular audits to verify compliance.
- Immediate correction of deviations through technical adjustments or training.
- Frequent review of the policy to adapt to emerging threats and ensure ongoing resilience.
