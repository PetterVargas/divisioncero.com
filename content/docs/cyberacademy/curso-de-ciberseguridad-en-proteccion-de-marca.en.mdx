## What is this course for?

In this space we'll see how to protect the digital reputation and identity of the organization and its employees, and why not, our personal brand too.

## What will I learn?

- 🚀 **Digital Brand Protection Fundamentals**: Understand the reputational impact of security incidents.
- 🚀 **Brand Impersonation and Fraud**: Recognize fake sites, bogus profiles and phishing that uses your brand.
- 🚀 **Brand Threat Monitoring**: Detect fraud campaigns that use your brand in time.
- 🚀 **Corporate Social Media Management**: Protect the access and credentials of your corporate accounts.
- 🚀 **Brand Incident Response**: Learn to coordinate the takedown of fake content and crisis communication.
