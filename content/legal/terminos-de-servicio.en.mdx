Paid plans are **recurring** subscriptions, billed monthly or yearly as you choose when you subscribe (the Medallas plan is offered with yearly billing only). Charges are non-refundable unless otherwise specified. You can change or cancel your plan at any time; changes and cancellation take effect at the end of the current billing cycle. Prices do not include the taxes that apply in your jurisdiction.

Prices are set in **US dollars (USD)**. Depending on your location, our payment provider may show the charge in your local currency, converted at the exchange rate of the day it is made; the local-currency amount may vary between charges, and your bank may apply its own conversion fees.

## 6. Base SKUs and additional usage

Each paid CSaaS and Self-hosting plan includes the **base SKUs** described on its pricing card. Usage beyond the base SKUs of your plan is billed as an additional charge on the **next invoice of your subscription** and charged automatically to the payment method on file. We will send you the usage detail before each additional charge.

The Starter plan is meant for companies of up to 50 employees and the Pro plan for companies of more than 50 employees or that use single sign-on (SSO). The platform does not block these capabilities: if we detect that your company goes beyond the scope of its plan, we will contact you to adjust it.

## 7. When a plan is cancelled or expires

When a CSaaS subscription ends, your company's data is kept and becomes read-only in the modules that require a paid plan; it becomes editable again once a plan is active. Integrations (API, MCP and webhooks) are unavailable while there is no active plan. Certificates issued during a CyberAcademy subscription remain valid.

Delivery times, geographic coverage and any shipping or customs costs not included are shared when each request is arranged.

## 9. Self-hosting

The Community edition is open source and free for organizations of up to 9 employees with annual revenue of up to 100K USD. Paid Self-hosting plans are bought from a company's account on the platform and activate the premium features of your installation: activation is tied to the **email address of the owner** of that company, verified with a code sent to that address, and the installation periodically checks that the subscription is still active. If the subscription ends, the installation returns to the Community edition without deleting your data. You are responsible for operating, securing and backing up your own infrastructure.
