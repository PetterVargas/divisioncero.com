---
title: "Business Cases for Creating a SOC"
description: "The business case behind creating a SOC"
---

Before investing in technology or staff, any serious organization needs to justify why it should create a SOC instead of simply buying more point controls. The business case translates cybersecurity risk into language leadership understands, comparing the cost of operating a continuous monitoring center with the expected cost of incidents not detected in time, including regulatory fines, operational disruption and reputational damage. In practice, the most common mistake is presenting the SOC as a compliance expense instead of a capability that reduces the exposure time to an active attacker in the network. A solid business case includes sector benchmark metrics, an analysis of current visibility gaps and a phased cost model, which makes it possible to negotiate budget incrementally instead of demanding the whole investment at once.
