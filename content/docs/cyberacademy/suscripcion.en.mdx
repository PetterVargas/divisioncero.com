## Open, open-source content

All CyberAcademy courses are **open, open-source content**. You can learn, share and reuse the material without paying anything.

## How do we sustain ourselves?

We sustain CyberAcademy through **assessments**, **certificates** and **diplomas of completion**. With your subscription you validate what you learn and help us keep the courses open for everyone.
