The CIS Controls v8 are a prioritized set of cyber defense actions developed by the Center for Internet Security. They group best practices into 18 controls with 153 safeguards distributed across 3 implementation groups according to the organization's maturity. Kudo maps its controls to the framework and the ISMS policies.
