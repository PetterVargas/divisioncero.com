---
title: "Algorithmic Bias and Its Impact on Security and Trust"
description: "Algorithmic bias and its impact on trust"
---

A model trained on historical data can reproduce and amplify the biases present in that data, systematically favoring or disadvantaging certain groups without any explicit intention to discriminate. From a security perspective, bias isn't just an ethical problem but an operational risk: a biased fraud detection system can generate disproportionate false positives in certain customer segments, eroding trust in the system and creating unnecessary friction, while bias not detected in time can expose the organization to regulatory penalties or reputational damage. Detecting it requires measuring the model's performance broken down by relevant subgroups, not just overall, and setting fairness thresholds as part of the acceptance criteria before a deployment. Ignoring this dimension makes any AI governance program incomplete, however robust its technical controls.
