---
title: "Separating Personal and Corporate Use (BYOD)"
description: "How to balance work and personal life on the same phone"
---

More and more organizations allow employees to use their personal phone for work, a model known as BYOD, which is convenient but mixes two worlds that are best kept separate. When the same device stores both your family photos and corporate email or company apps, a security incident on one side can end up affecting the other, and the organization needs a certain level of control over data that belongs to it even if it's on your personal device. Many companies solve this with device management apps that create a separate, encrypted space just for work, without touching your photos or personal apps, or with independent work profiles within the same phone. It's worth understanding what access your employer has to the device under this policy, for example whether it can remotely wipe only corporate data or the whole phone, so you can use your phone with confidence and without surprises.
