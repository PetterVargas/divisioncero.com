## Signing up with email

To access the DivisionCero platform you need to **sign up with an email address** at [app.divisioncero.com/auth/sign-up](https://app.divisioncero.com/auth/sign-up).

<Cards>
  <Card title="Sign up" description="Create your account with your email address." href="https://app.divisioncero.com/auth/sign-up" />
</Cards>

## Logging in with an OTP code

Our authentication model uses an **OTP (One-Time Password)**, a single-use code:

1. Enter your email address on the platform.
2. Check your inbox: we'll send you a code.
3. Enter that code as your **login code**.

That's why you need to **have access to your email** every time you log in.

<Callout title="We don't store passwords">
  Since you log in with a one-time code, you don't need to create or remember a password, and we **don't store passwords**.
</Callout>
