The Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard (PCI DSS) v4.0 is the global security standard for organizations that process, store or transmit payment card data. It defines 12 requirements organized into 6 control objectives. Kudo maps its requirements and sub-requirements to the framework and the ISMS policies.
