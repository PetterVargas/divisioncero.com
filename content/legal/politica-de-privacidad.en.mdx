Your privacy matters a lot to us. This policy explains how we collect, use and protect your information when you use our platform.

## 1. Information we collect

**Personal information:** name, email address and billing details, collected to create your account, process transactions and provide support.

**Usage data:** IP addresses, browser characteristics and behavior patterns, which we use to improve platform performance and maintain security.

## 2. How we use your information

We use the information we collect to provide and improve our services, send you updates and respond to support requests, and comply with legal obligations.

## 3. Data sharing

We share information with service providers that handle payments, analytics and hosting; with legal authorities when required by law; and with strategic partners who help improve our service offering. **We never sell your data to third parties.**

## 4. Your rights

You have the right to access your data, correct inaccuracies, delete your account and opt out of marketing communications.

## 5. Data security

We protect your information with industry standards, including encryption, secure servers and periodic audits.

## 6. Retention policy

We keep your data only for as long as necessary to provide the service or comply with legal obligations.

## 7. Cookie policy

For more information about our use of cookies, see our [Cookie Policy](/en/legal/politica-de-cookies).

## 8. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We will notify you by email or within the platform of any significant change to this policy.

## 9. Contact us

If you have privacy-related questions, you can contact us through our official channels.
