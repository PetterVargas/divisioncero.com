---
title: "Communicating Compliance to the Organization"
description: "Making compliance visible day to day"
---

Regulatory compliance is often perceived within the organization as an issue exclusive to the legal or risk team, and that perception is precisely what needs to be broken for controls to hold up in daily operations, because business teams are the ones who actually carry out most of the relevant controls, not the compliance area. Communicating compliance to the organization means translating regulatory requirements into concrete, relevant messages for each audience, explaining not only what they must do differently but why it matters, avoiding purely legal language that creates disconnection and rejection. In practice, this is achieved through recurring channels such as short newsletters when there are relevant regulatory changes, training sessions targeted at specific roles instead of generic talks for the whole company, and publicly recognizing the areas that keep their controls up to date, thereby reinforcing a culture where compliance is seen as part of doing the job well and not as an external burden.
