---
title: "Separating Personal and Corporate Environments"
description: "Separate containers on the same device"
---

Separating personal and corporate environments consists of creating isolated containers or profiles within the same device, so the company's apps, data and security policies don't mix with the employee's personal use, a technique especially relevant in BYOD schemes where the same phone serves both purposes. Its practical importance lies in the fact that this separation lets the company apply encryption, remote wipe and data copy restrictions only to the corporate container without touching the user's photos, messages or personal apps, thus avoiding the natural rejection that full-control policies over personal devices create. A concrete example is an employee who resigns from the company, where the IT department can remotely wipe only the corporate email, documents and apps from their personal phone, without affecting their family photos or private apps at all.
