---
title: "Backup and Recovery Policy"
description: "Backup and recovery of critical business information"
---

## Purpose
Ensure that data, systems and software configurations are protected against loss, disruption or security incidents, guaranteeing the integrity and availability of critical information.

## Scope
Applies to all the organization's systems, data and software configurations, including servers, workstations, databases and cloud services, and to the staff, vendors and consultants who interact with them.

## Objectives
- Back up data, systems and configurations regularly and securely.
- Provide fast recovery in the event of data loss or corruption.
- Periodically validate the effectiveness of backups.
- Regularly review and update the associated cybersecurity requirements.

## Standards

**Data Backup**
- Identify critical data with the Head of IT.
- Store backups in physically separate locations (DR Site).
- Determine backup frequency according to criticality: daily, weekly or monthly.
- Use full, incremental or differential backups as needed.
- Store on secure, encrypted and protected media, including the cloud.
- Retain backups for at least 35 days or longer depending on criticality.
- Verify backups at least twice a year and perform periodic recovery tests.

**Data Recovery**
- Document backup and recovery procedures (backup types, frequency, owners, locations and recovery techniques).
- Perform restore tests at least quarterly.
- Report results to the Head of IT and Information Security.

**Secure Storage**
- Protect backups with physical and electronic controls (locks, ACS, biometrics).
- Keep access logs for at least 30 days.
- Implement environmental security: fire detection and suppression, electrical protection, CCTV.

**Backup Testing**
- Define the scope, develop disaster scenarios, run tests and analyze results.
- Update procedures based on findings and document lessons learned.

**Storage Management**
- Transfer data between media according to cost and accessibility.
- Ensure critical backups are kept on fast, reliable devices.

**Business Continuity and Recovery**
- Maintain a documented and tested BCDRP, including:
  - Identification of risks and critical functions.
  - Emergency response team.
  - Backup procedures, alternate sites and communication protocols.
  - Regular testing and updates based on lessons learned.

**Data Retention**
- Corporate data: 1 year.
- Customer data: according to regulatory requirements.
- Employment data: 1 year from termination.

## Monitoring and Review
- Review the policy at least annually or after significant changes in IT or security.
- Management and Information Security oversee the program, ensuring alignment with the Information Security Management System and applicable regulations.
