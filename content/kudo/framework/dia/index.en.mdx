## Domain Description

Dedicated to the **security, privacy and governance of organizational data, as well as the specific controls 
for the responsible use of Artificial Intelligence systems**. It covers the *data inventory, the mapping of collection 
points, data lifecycle management, security controls over sensitive information, retention 
periods and the right to be forgotten, the geographic location of data, and the risks associated with AI models*. 
It treats **data as the organization's most valuable asset ("the crown jewel")**, integrating privacy controls 
and promoting the use of AI without exposing data to unauthorized retraining.
