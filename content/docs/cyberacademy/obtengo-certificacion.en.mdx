## Yes, you get a certification

The **certification** is on your **diploma**. Anyone can validate it online using the **QR code** or the **certification code** that appears on the diploma.

If you want to see your certifications, go to [app.divisioncero.com/home/certificaciones](https://app.divisioncero.com/home/certificaciones).
