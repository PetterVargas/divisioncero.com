---
title: "How to Model Secure Behaviors from the Top"
description: "How leadership models safe habits by example"
---

Modeling secure behaviors from the top means executives don't just approve security policies but visibly follow them in their daily work, because employees tend to replicate what they see their leaders do far more than what they read in an internal policy manual. This principle stems from a simple reality: if a manager shares their screen in a video call with passwords visible in digital sticky notes, or asks their assistant to use their own session to access restricted systems, they're sending a much stronger message than any formal security training. A concrete, effective example is for leadership to actively use a password manager and mention it openly in team meetings, thus normalizing a tool many employees consider unnecessary until they see their own leaders use it without exception.
