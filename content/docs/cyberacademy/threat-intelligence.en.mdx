## What is this course for?

Intelligence sources, IoCs and operational integration.

## What will I learn?

- 🚀 **Threat Intelligence Fundamentals**: Learn the levels and lifecycle of threat intelligence.
- 🚀 **Information Sources and Collection**: Collect indicators of compromise from open and commercial sources.
- 🚀 **Threat Analysis**: Model adversaries and analyze campaigns with MITRE ATT&CK and the Diamond Model.
- 🚀 **Intelligence Production and Dissemination**: Produce reports and integrate them into detection tools.
- 🚀 **Strategic Application of Intelligence**: Use threat intelligence to prioritize risks and decisions.
