---
title: "Formal Charter and Executive Sponsorship"
description: "The team's legitimacy and executive backing"
---

A CSIRT without clear executive backing lacks the authority it needs to act during an incident: requesting the isolation of a production server or suspending a deployment requires someone with decision-making power to have previously endorsed that capability. The formal charter is the document that officially creates the team, defines its existence within the org chart and establishes who sponsors it at the leadership level, usually a CISO, CTO or risk committee. This sponsorship must translate into an assigned budget, authority to escalate critical decisions and an explicit mandate before the rest of the organization. Without this step, the team risks operating informally, with its authority questioned at the moment it's needed most: in the middle of a real crisis.
