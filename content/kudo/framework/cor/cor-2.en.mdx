---
title: COR-2 - Regulatory Compliance Mapping
description: Comprehensive management control for legal, regulatory and contractual obligations
---

## Control Definition

All legal, regulatory and contractual obligations applicable to the organization must be mapped and documented, classified and assigned a compliance owner, and regulatory changes must be monitored to keep the inventory up to date. A matrix must be built that links each obligation to the implemented cybersecurity controls, and any compliance gaps detected must be identified and closed.

## Requirements

### Requirement 1

We map and document, in the legal context, all obligations applicable to the organization in the countries where we operate.

### Requirement 2

We map and document, in the regulatory context, all obligations applicable to the organization in the countries where we operate.

### Requirement 3

We map and document, in the contractual context, all applicable obligations based on our customers' terms and conditions.

### Requirement 4

We classify legal, regulatory and contractual obligations by jurisdiction and type of obligation.

### Requirement 5

We assign a compliance owner for each regulation identified in the inventory.

### Requirement 6

We define the review frequency and record the current compliance status of each obligation in the inventory.

### Requirement 7

We build a compliance matrix that maps each legal, regulatory and contractual requirement to the implemented cybersecurity controls.

### Requirement 8

We monitor legal, regulatory and contractual changes on a scheduled basis and update the inventory.

### Requirement 9

We identify legal, regulatory and contractual compliance gaps and create action plans to close them.
