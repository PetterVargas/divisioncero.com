## What is this course for?

Team, how important is the role of leaders in security culture and informed decision-making to you? When we analyze the answer, it explains much of the purpose of this course.

## What will I learn?

- 🚀 **Cybersecurity as a Strategic Priority**: Understand why leadership is key to a solid security culture.
- 🚀 **Governance and Risk Management**: Define roles and assess the cyber risks your organization faces.
- 🚀 **Incident Decision-Making**: Prepare to lead the response to security incidents and crises.
- 🚀 **Leadership and Organizational Culture**: Model secure behaviors and strengthen your team's security culture.
- 🚀 **Cybersecurity in the Value Chain**: Manage the cyber risk introduced by third parties and business partners.
