---
title: "Internal Communication during an Active Incident"
description: "Keeping internal teams informed"
---

Internal communication during an active incident determines whether the organization responds in a coordinated way or whether each team acts on its own with incomplete or contradictory information. In practice, this requires predefined channels separate from the possibly compromised environment, since communicating through the same corporate email or chat the attacker could be monitoring is a mistake that has given away entire response strategies in real incidents. It's also key to establish a cadence of updates to leadership and the teams involved, even when there's no relevant news, because silence creates anxiety and rushed decisions among those waiting for information. An important nuance is limiting communication to those who really need to know, following the need-to-know principle, since spreading sensitive technical details across the whole organization can leak information outside or cause unnecessary panic among employees who have no active role in the response.
