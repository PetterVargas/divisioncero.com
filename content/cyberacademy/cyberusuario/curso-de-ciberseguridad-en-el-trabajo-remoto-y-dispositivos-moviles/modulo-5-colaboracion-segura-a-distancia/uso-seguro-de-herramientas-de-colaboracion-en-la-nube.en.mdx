---
title: "Secure Use of Cloud Collaboration Tools"
description: "How to work as a team without exposing information"
---

Cloud collaboration tools, such as shared documents, task boards or team chats, make remote work enormously easier, but they also multiply the places where company information can be exposed if they aren't configured carefully. A frequent mistake is leaving a document with edit permissions open to anyone with the link, instead of restricting it to the specific people who really need it, which turns an internal file into something practically public. It's also worth periodically reviewing which third-party integrations have access to these platforms, since apps connected long ago and forgotten may still have permissions over sensitive information without anyone remembering. Using only the tools approved by the organization, instead of personal solutions for convenience, and checking the access level before sharing any file are simple habits that prevent leaks that are later hard to trace and fix.
