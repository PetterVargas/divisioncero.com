---
title: "Security Policies for Employee Devices"
description: "Clear rules for acceptable use and responsibility"
---

Security policies for employee devices are the set of formal rules that define how corporate devices must be used, what software may be installed, how passwords are managed and which behaviors are prohibited, such as connecting the device to public networks without a VPN or sharing credentials among coworkers. Their practical value isn't in the document itself, but in translating technical security expectations into rules that people without cybersecurity training can understand, and in serving as a legal basis to require compliance and act on serious violations. An important nuance is that a policy nobody knows or that's too restrictive for daily work ends up being ignored in practice, so its effectiveness depends as much on actively communicating it and training employees as on designing it realistically for how the organization actually works.
