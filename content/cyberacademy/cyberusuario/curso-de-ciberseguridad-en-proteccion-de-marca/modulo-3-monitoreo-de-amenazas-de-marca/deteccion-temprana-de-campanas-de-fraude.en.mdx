---
title: "Early Detection of Fraud Campaigns"
description: "How to identify fraud campaigns before they escalate"
---

Detecting a fraud campaign early means recognizing the initial signs, such as a sudden increase in customer complaints, fake ads using the company's logo or mass messages offering promotions the brand never launched. The sooner the pattern is identified, the faster you can act to limit the damage, because these campaigns usually grow exponentially in a matter of hours through social networks and messaging apps. Having clear processes for escalating alerts internally, combined with active monitoring of mentions and suspicious domains, makes the difference between stopping a fraud when it affects a few people or discovering it when it has already caused financial losses and significant reputational damage for thousands of customers.
