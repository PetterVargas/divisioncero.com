## Domain Description

Domain covering the **comprehensive cybersecurity of the organization's networks, servers, technology platforms and critical 
assets**. It includes the management of *cloud service providers (CSPs)*, the *asset inventory*, 
*network and system security controls*, *information and data encryption*, and the administration of *cryptographic keys*. 
It promotes network segmentation, access restriction and the design of architectures with a minimal attack 
surface, following the **KISS (Keep It Simple and Secure)** principle. It addresses *infrastructure as the most 
attacked component of the organization's technology ecosystem*.
