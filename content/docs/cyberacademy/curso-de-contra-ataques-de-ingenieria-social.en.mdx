## What is this course for?

Here we'll learn what can happen because of a mistake on our part, whom we can affect with a click made without checking, what losses we can cause; simply put, to be aware and empathetic in digital environments.

## What will I learn?

- 🚀 **Social Engineering Fundamentals**: Understand how and why social engineering attacks work.
- 🚀 **Phishing and Its Variants**: Recognize phishing and its variants by email, SMS and phone calls.
- 🚀 **In-Person and Digital Manipulation Techniques**: Identify pretexting, baiting and other manipulation techniques.
- 🚀 **Warning Signs and Verification**: Learn to verify suspicious requests before acting.
- 🚀 **Responding to a Social Engineering Attack**: Learn the steps to take if you fell for a social engineering scam.

## Projects

With this course you'll create, optimize, automate or improve the following projects:

- 🚀 You'll learn to identify phishing emails and fraudulent websites.
- 🚀 You'll learn to detect and prevent social engineering.
- 🚀 You'll learn about the losses some companies suffered because of employees who didn't check before clicking.
