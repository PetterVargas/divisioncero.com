---
title: "Evaluating the Effectiveness of the Implemented Model"
description: "Measuring whether Zero Trust meets its real goal"
---

Evaluating the effectiveness of the implemented Zero Trust model means going beyond confirming that the tools are installed and verifying with concrete data whether they're really reducing risk, through metrics such as incident detection and containment time, the number of anomalous accesses blocked, or the results of internal attack simulation exercises. In practice this evaluation matters because a poorly calibrated Zero Trust architecture can create a false sense of security, with policies so permissive they block nothing relevant or so restrictive that teams bypass them through uncontrolled exceptions. A common method is to run breach simulation exercises, where an assessor is given legitimate but limited access and the team measures how far they can move laterally before being detected, which precisely reveals whether segmentation and continuous verification are working as designed.
