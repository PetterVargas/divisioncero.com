The Secure Development Course is part of the CyberUsuario learning path; we wrote this description to give you a 
mind map of what we're achieving and how everything is part of an ecosystem focused on continuous improvement.

This is one of our most technical spaces in the CyberUsuario learning path, and it's aimed at anyone who 
works directly or indirectly with code or software development, but only from the perspective of an employee getting ahead of problems; 
there are other courses in our CyberGuardián learning path for protecting the software development lifecycle (SDLC) in terms of management 
and governance. Here the idea is to get well ahead of Security SDLC controls: it's really about what I can do for cybersecurity as a 
developer on my team before pushing my changes to a remote branch.
