## What is this course for?

Classification, DLP and protection of sensitive data.

## What will I learn?

- 🚀 **Data Security Fundamentals**: Classify data according to its sensitivity and learn its lifecycle.
- 🚀 **Protecting Data at Rest and in Transit**: Encrypt, tokenize and mask sensitive data at rest and in transit.
- 🚀 **Data Access Control**: Apply least privilege and audit access to critical data.
- 🚀 **Data Loss Prevention (DLP)**: Monitor data flows and respond to information exposure incidents.
- 🚀 **Data Governance**: Define data governance roles and retention and quality policies.

## Projects

With this course you'll create, optimize, automate or improve the following projects:

- 🚀 You'll implement data classification and protection systems with automated DLP and encryption in transit.
- 🚀 You'll configure differential privacy systems and anonymization techniques for regulatory compliance.
