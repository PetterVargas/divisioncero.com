## What is this course for?

Integrating security into CI/CD pipelines and DevSecOps practices.

## What will I learn?

- 🚀 **AppSec and DevSecOps Fundamentals**: Learn AppSec principles and the Shift Left model in development.
- 🚀 **Code Security**: Identify OWASP Top 10 vulnerabilities with SAST and dependency management.
- 🚀 **CI/CD Pipeline Security**: Integrate security controls and gates into your CI/CD pipeline.
- 🚀 **Dynamic and Security Testing**: Apply DAST, IAST and penetration testing before deployment.
- 🚀 **AppSec Culture and Governance**: Drive an AppSec culture and measure your program's maturity.

## Projects

With this course you'll create, optimize, automate or improve the following projects:

- 🚀 You'll implement CI/CD pipelines with automated vulnerability analysis (SAST/DAST).
