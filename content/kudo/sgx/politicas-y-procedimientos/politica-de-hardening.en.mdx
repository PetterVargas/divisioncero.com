---
title: "Hardening Policy"
description: "Secure configuration and hardening of systems and applications"
---

## Purpose
Ensure information security in cloud and on-premises environments, protecting data, ensuring confidentiality, integrity and availability, and aligning with the organization's standards.

## Scope
Applies to all the organization's employees and systems in the cloud and in physical facilities.

## Access and Accounts
- Only authorized employees (IT, security and management) access systems and services.
- Mandatory use of strong passwords and MFA.
- Token-based access must be rotated every 180 days.
- SSH access to servers through a bastion and IP restrictions.

## Infrastructure and Servers
- Ubuntu servers updated regularly; separate volumes for the OS and data (encrypted).
- Changes subject to change management.
- Strong password policies on all systems.
- Automatic monitoring of updates and weekly patches.

## Network Security
- Network segregation by trust, sensitivity and criticality.
- Use of a single VPC per environment, a WAF and a load balancer for HTTP/HTTPS.
- Segregated wireless networks; guest Wi-Fi with strict restrictions.
- Network access monitoring and control, authentication and connection encryption.

## Databases and Cryptography
- Encrypted RDS databases in the designated region, daily backups, 15-day retention.
- Encryption keys managed by the cloud provider's KMS.
- AES-256 encryption in transit and at rest.

## Patch and Vulnerability Management
- Monthly patch cycle.
- Regular vulnerability scanning with standard tools.
- Critical vulnerabilities addressed immediately with a documented remediation plan.

## Penetration and Resilience Testing
- Annual penetration tests or after significant changes.
- Advanced threat-led testing every three years, including critical systems in production.
- Vulnerability assessments before deploying critical services.
- Findings reports and a remediation plan for critical gaps.

## Data Loss Prevention (DLP)
- Identification and classification of sensitive data.
- Monitoring of channels such as email, devices and file transfers.
- Restriction of copying, pasting and uploading to external services.
- Protection of backups with encryption and access control.

## Threat Intelligence
- Participation in threat and vulnerability analysis from trusted sources.
- Use of information for proactive security decisions.

## Monitoring and Review
- Periodic compliance and permission audits.
- Monitoring of logs, security events and vulnerabilities.
- Annual review of the policy and adjustments according to threats or environmental changes.
- Immediate reporting of incidents to the information security team.
