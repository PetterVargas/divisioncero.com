The Social Engineering Defense Course is part of the CyberUsuario learning path; we wrote this description to give you a 
mind map of what we're achieving and how everything is part of an ecosystem focused on continuous improvement. 

If you're here, you most likely clicked, provided data or were vulnerable in an internal cybersecurity exercise. This space 
isn't about learning the types of attacks or threats; it's 100% about being aware and empathetic, because a trivial mistake has affected 
many environments, and we're going to show you some of them.
