## What does each certification include?

When you successfully complete a course, you'll receive:

- 📜 **Digital certificate** with a unique verification code
- 🎯 **Validation of competencies** in the specific course or domain
- 📊 **Record of projects completed** during the course
- 🔗 **Digital badge** to share on LinkedIn and professional networks
- ⏰ **Issue date** and completed version.

## Full Learning Path Certification

Each course has its own 🏆 **specific certification**; in addition, when you complete **all the courses** in a learning path, you'll get a special certification:

- 🏆 **CyberUsuario Certified** - When you complete the 5 courses of the CyberUsuario learning path
- 🏆 **CyberGuardián Certified** - When you complete the 8 domains of the CyberGuardianes learning path. In addition, each domain has its own certification:
  - 🏅 **Organizational Coherence (COR) Certification** - When you complete the domain's courses
  - 🏅 **Identity and Endpoint Cybersecurity (CIP) Certification** - When you complete the domain's courses
  - 🏅 **Infrastructure Cybersecurity (CIF) Certification** - When you complete the domain's courses
  - 🏅 **Application Cybersecurity (CAP) Certification** - When you complete the domain's courses
  - 🏅 **Business Continuity and Change (CCN) Certification** - When you complete the domain's courses
  - 🏅 **Human Talent and Vendor Cybersecurity (THP) Certification** - When you complete the domain's courses
  - 🏅 **Data and Artificial Intelligence Cybersecurity (DIA) Certification** - When you complete the domain's courses
  - 🏅 **Cybersecurity Analysis, Detection and Response (ADR) Certification** - When you complete the domain's courses

## Certification Coin

Our courses are **free** and our certifications are **low cost**. In addition to the digital certification, you can get your **certification coin**: a physical coin that commemorates your achievement.

- 🪙 The subscription **doesn't include** the certification coin; it's purchased separately.
- ✅ Coins are **only sold** to people who hold a **valid certification**.
