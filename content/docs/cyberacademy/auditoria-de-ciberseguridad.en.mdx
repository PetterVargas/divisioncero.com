## What is this course for?

Planning, execution and follow-up of security audits.

## What will I learn?

- 🚀 **Cybersecurity Audit Fundamentals**: Learn the purpose and approach of a risk-based cybersecurity audit.
- 🚀 **Audit Planning**: Prioritize the audit plan according to the risk of critical processes.
- 🚀 **Audit Execution**: Collect evidence and assess the effectiveness of the controls in place.
- 🚀 **Reporting Findings**: Classify findings and communicate actionable results to leadership.
- 🚀 **Follow-up and Continuous Improvement**: Follow up on remediation plans and assess the program's maturity.

## Projects

With this course you'll create, optimize, automate or improve the following projects:

- 🚀 Continuous improvement and corrective actions with AI.
- 🚀 You'll rely on AI for the annual compliance review.
