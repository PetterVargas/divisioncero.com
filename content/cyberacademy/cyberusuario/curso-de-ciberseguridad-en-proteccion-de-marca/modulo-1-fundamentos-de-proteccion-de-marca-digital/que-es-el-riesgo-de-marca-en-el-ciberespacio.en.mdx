---
title: "What Brand Risk in Cyberspace Is"
description: "How the digital environment threatens corporate reputation"
---

Brand risk in cyberspace refers to all the ways a company's reputation can be damaged through the internet, from fake profiles on social networks to websites that impersonate it to scam customers. This risk matters because consumer trust today is built and destroyed online, often faster than the organization can react; a viral post about a counterfeit product or a data leak can undo years of work in a matter of hours. Understanding this risk from the marketing or communications area is key because, although the technical attack happens on a server, the brand team is the one that faces the public consequences and responds to upset customers.
