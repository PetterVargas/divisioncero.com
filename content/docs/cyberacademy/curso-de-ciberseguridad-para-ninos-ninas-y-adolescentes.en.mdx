## What is this course for?

Champions, this is because of you and for you. Let's start with empathy toward our classmates: being behind a computer or phone doesn't give us the right to destroy friends, which is why we designed the course with the following modules:

## What will I learn?

- 🚀 **My Life on the Internet**: Discover what the internet is and why your personal information is a treasure.
- 🚀 **Taking Care of My Information**: Learn to create strong passwords and take care of your privacy online.
- 🚀 **Recognizing Danger**: Identify strangers, warning signs and cyberbullying.
- 🚀 **Acting Safely**: Learn to ask for help, block and report when something makes you uncomfortable.
- 🚀 **Good Digital Habits**: Build healthy, respectful screen habits.

## Projects

With this course you'll create, optimize, automate or improve the following projects:

- 🚀 You'll identify risks such as cyberbullying, grooming and sextortion, and you'll know whom to ask for help.
- 🚀 You'll take care of your digital footprint and your online reputation.
